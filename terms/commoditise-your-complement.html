Commoditise your complement

first seen 2026-W34 · 8 links · 1 min read

The strategy of giving away the thing next to what you sell so that demand for your product grows: Nvidia releasing open models and data to sell chips, Meta flooding the zone with tokens to hurt token-sellers. Lambert's framing of who pays for open models.

Also seen as commoditize the complement, flooding the zone with tokens

How the sources use it

  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-04) Thompson's frame: partners above commoditise the model, OpenAI commoditises the chip below; Brockman does not adopt the word. Cross-source (Interconnects).
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-08-17) Nvidia and Meta doing it in different ways.

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.
