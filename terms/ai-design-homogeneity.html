AI design homogeneity

first seen 2026-W35 · 3 links · 1 min read

The tendency of AI-generated software and interfaces to converge on the same default look, layouts and choices, so that AI-built products become recognisable by their sameness. Berman's design-side counterpart to Orosz's observation about AI-homogenised writing.

Also seen as AI design smell, forest green default, AI design sameness

How the sources use it

  • Rojin Ziaei (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Matthew Berman, Gergely Orosz); the same convergence-on-one-output pattern the vault has tracked in interfaces and prose, measured here as a ratio of standard deviations.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-04) forest green and flat design by default.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-03) the persistent default look across projects; echoes the AI-writing homogenisation theme in Orosz's notes.
  • Karuna Nandkumar, anonymous contributor (ChinaTalk, 2026-08-28) the authors' observation that US and PLA cartoon drone animations converge in shape and style. Cross-source (Matthew Berman).
