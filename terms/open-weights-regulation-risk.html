Open weights regulation risk

first seen 2026-W35 · 10 links · 1 min read

The risk that regulation sidelines open-weights models: David Sacks, relayed in Berman's daily, argues that industry-backed standards and post-release monitoring requirements would favour closed systems without any explicit ban, and Lambert expects an intentional hack using an open model to bring severe restrictions on stronger open models. Lambert's reading list gathers the policy side of the question, including his argument that vague federal oversight sets up a clash with, or ban of, frontier open models, and lawmakers' probes of companies using Chinese models.
