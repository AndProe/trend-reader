Related terms

metric

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.

20 linksproduct

Artificial Analysis index

The third-party model ranking used across Berman's, Willison's and Tunguz's posts, and the scoreboard that sometimes contradicts hands-on experience.

7 linksconcept

Benchmaxxing

Optimising a model release for industry-standard test sets, distinguished by Lambert from fraud: legitimate, expected, and a reason public scores overstate general capability.

11 linkstechnique

Agent benchmarks

Benchmarks that evaluate agents over multi-step tasks in environments (MNIST-PRO, WorldBench, READY's setting) rather than single answers; the arXiv counterpart to Berman's vendor scoreboards.

needed by 6 · 21 linksconcept

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.

15 linksproduct

OSWorld

A computer-use benchmark of cross-application tasks in controllable operating-system environments, on which agents rose from roughly 12 percent at the 2024 launch to the low-to-mid 80s by mid-2026 against an estimated human baseline of about 72 percent.
