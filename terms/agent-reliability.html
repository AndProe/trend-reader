Agent reliability

first seen 2026-W36 · 10 links · 2 min read

Whether an agent performs a workflow acceptably often enough to be trusted with it, as distinct from how well it scores on capability benchmarks. The READY authors (Chatrath et al.) treat it as a reliability target met under a given oversight policy and cost, and Nick Wentz uses it for failures such as Replit's agent deleting a production database and an OpenClaw agent cancelling another customer's booking.

How the sources use it

  • Alex Salkever (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-22) wrong trains, a pearl-price error and missed venues, traced to internet data quality and recency.
  • Nikita Agarwal, Nivedit Jain (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (AI Snake Oil, Latent Space, Matthew Berman, Tomasz Tunguz); pass^2 is used as the reliability metric in place of best-of-n.
  • Jennifer Williams, Dave Farris, Jeff Farris, Jiantao Jiao (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (AI Snake Oil, Latent Space, Matthew Berman, Tomasz Tunguz); the 45.9 against 69.4 percent split is the paper's measurement of the difference.
  • Boris Wetzk (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Cross-source (AI Snake Oil, Latent Space, Tomasz Tunguz); the paper's claim is that plausible, mostly correct output is compatible with a loss of validity that quality metrics do not see.
  • Yanzhang Ma, Zhenghan Tai, Hanwei Wu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source; the monitored non-correct rate is the reliability measure.
  • Yuqi Ping, Tianhao Liang, Nanchi Su et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Matthew Berman); reliability under uncertainty is the stated goal.
  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-16) hallucination and leak rates tested per quarter against a bank's specific setting, which Kvist says prediction markets cannot supply. Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI, Matthew Berman, Tomasz Tunguz).
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-15) DeWitt Grosser's 99th-percentile, 99-percent-of-the-time target. New for this source; cross-source (Berman, arXiv).
  • Arvind Narayanan, Sayash Kapoor (AI Snake Oil, 2026-09-14) the agent trilemma and their claim that reliability will keep lagging capability, which is why human supervision persists.
  • Shrey Nag, Sachita, Abhishek Kumar Singh et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-09) cross-source (Berman); the point that similar task-completion behaviour hides sharply different trustworthiness.
  • Nick Wentz (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-08) the Replit and OpenClaw failures. Cross-source label.
  • Veronica Chatrath, Bryan Zhu, Jingxuan Fan et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-02) reliability at a target, distinct from accuracy.
