Muse Spark

first seen 2026-W34 · 5 links · 1 min read

Data flywheel

Inference usage feeding model improvement that attracts more usage; in Tunguz's ads-model post the mechanism Meta buys cheaply by pricing prompts as data.

7 linksconcept

Frontier models

The strongest available models at any moment, now gated rather than openly sold and bypassed by most real usage; the tier the open models chase and the reference point for every pricing and access argument in the vault.

needed by 7 · 21 linksconcept

Two-tier data pricing

Selling the same model at two prices: a Standard tier on which the lab promises never to train on prompts and completions, and a Contributor tier about 92 to 95 percent cheaper in exchange for training rights, as Meta did with Muse Spark 1.3 at $1.25/$4.25 against $0.10/$0.20 per million tokens.

Muse Glimmer

Meta's 30B-parameter open-weights on-device model, a return to open releases after several closed launches, distinct from the closed Muse Spark.
