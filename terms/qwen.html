Qwen

first seen 2026-W34 · 5 links · 1 min read

Alibaba's open-weight model family, the most downloaded on Hugging Face and the most common base for derivative models. Will Raphaelson uses it as the adoption evidence for Chinese open models, while Florian Brand tracks its releases, from the Qwen3.8-Flash-Next architecture preview to the largest openly released Qwen, which he places under a custom licence and behind models of its size.

Also seen as Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B, Qwen4

Revenue-share licenses

Open-model licences that are free to experiment with and require a commercial agreement, security review or revenue share above a threshold (Kimi K3 at $20 million a year); 'open until you scale'.

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.
