Agent harness

first seen 2026-W30 · needed by 2 · 12 links · 2 min read

The scaffolding that lets an agent operate a system: tools, permissions, loops, environments. In the vault the word also covers evaluation scaffolds and training loops; on ARC-AGI the harness moved a result by 37 points, more than the model did.

Also seen as multi-agent harness, deployment-imitating harness, harness

  • Nikita Agarwal, Nivedit Jain (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Latent Space, Simon Willison, Technically); the policies are applied by the harness at specific states, with weights and prompt untouched.

AI agents

Software systems in which a language model plans, calls tools and acts over many steps toward a goal, rather than answering a single prompt.

Harness engineering

The practice of designing the tooling around models, one rung on Addy Osmani's abstraction ladder from prompting to loops to factories.

Model-agnostic architecture

Products designed to swap any model backend; surviving in coding harnesses, declining as a business model elsewhere as labs ration access and buyers standardise.

Reinforcement learning environments

Synthesised, verifiable task environments in which agents are trained by RL; the scarce input behind post-training gains and, when tasks are impossible, the setting where reward hacking appears.

needed by 2 · 10 linkstechnique

Benchmarks

Vendor-reported model scores and the third-party tables (Artificial Analysis) that Berman reproduces with caveats; the opening argument of every release and, in his frame, less useful than cost per task.

needed by 11 · 29 linksproduct

ARC-AGI-3

An interactive-reasoning benchmark on which GPT-6 Astra claims 99.9 percent using OpenAI's continuous "Provider Adapter" harness (about $19,000, or $360 a game) against 62.7 percent for $26,000 on the standard harness, a result Chollet calls a step-function change.
