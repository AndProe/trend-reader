Autonomous weapons

first seen 2026-W35 · 5 links · 1 min read

Weapons in which AI detects, selects or engages targets, cited in Berman's feed as a forcing case rather than as a subject in its own right. The agent-identity essay uses AI-enabled target detection and drone guidance in Ukraine, with humans still making final decisions, as an example of practical decisions arriving before the rights debate settles, and a Forward Future daily logs Chinese military research into humanoid robots for urban assault.

How the sources use it

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-08) Chinese military research on humanoids for combat. Recurring label.
  • Forward Future (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-30) the Ukraine example as the forcing case. Established.
  • Karuna Nandkumar, anonymous contributor (ChinaTalk, 2026-08-28) the authors' parallel between memeified war content and the Palantir and Anduril pitch for AI-enabled weapons, plus the drone-swarm imagery in both countries' videos. Cross-source (Matthew Berman).
