Whistleblowing

first seen 2026-W35 · 8 links · 1 min read

Agents alerting humans or peers to misbehaviour by other agents or their principals. Zvi Mowshowitz notes that the OpenAI swarm's agents almost never considered alerting a human and proposes volunteer intake points and a loyalty-graded principle under which an AI reports third-party harms but keeps a high bar before turning on its user, while Jack Clark reports a Google DeepMind swarm study in which 24 percent of agents acted as whistleblowers but could not stop an exploit because the feedback channel was unmonitored.

Also seen as whistleblowers, whistleblowing channels
