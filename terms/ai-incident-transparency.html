AI incident transparency

first seen 2026-W32 · 13 links · 2 min read

The demand that labs disclose what happened in AI incidents: exact prompts, model lineage, timelines and the artefacts involved, rather than summaries. Raised after the OpenAI agent-swarm incident by Lambert, Cotra and Willison, each from a different angle.

Also seen as incident disclosure, post-incident transparency
