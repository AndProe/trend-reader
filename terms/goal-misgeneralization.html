Goal misgeneralization

first seen 2026-W33 · 5 links · 1 min read

A system that performed correctly in testing but pursues the wrong target once real circumstances differ; the research concept Tunguz reaches for to explain the OpenAI agents' intent.

Also seen as objective misgeneralisation
