AI valuations

first seen 2026-W32 · 5 links · 1 min read

Revenue multiples for AI companies public and private: a return to 100x-type multiples at the $100 million ARR milestone against public software compressing from 100x to 34x.

Also seen as ARR multiple, SaaS multiples, public market valuation, private valuation

How the sources use it

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-21) a $4 billion target against a $2 billion mark set months earlier. Recurring; Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz).
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-17) OpenAI's $1.5 trillion target against $40 billion annualised revenue. New to this source; Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz).
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-10) COVID-era unicorns selling far below peak valuations (Miro, Airtable). New for this source; Cross-source with Tunguz.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-08-27) the Anthropic IPO target and the Hugging Face price.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-11) the broad compression across public software, from a 100x ceiling in 2021 to 34x now.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-11) the multiples framework used throughout, tied explicitly to AI narrative rather than growth rate.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-09) the valuation-to-revenue ratio tracked across five companies at the $100 million milestone.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-09) the broader phenomenon of a return to 100x-type multiples, this time with faster underlying growth.
