Complementary assets

first seen 2026-W34 · 4 links · 1 min read

Catalini's term for the scarce resources (compute, data, distribution) that capture a general-purpose technology's value instead of its inventors, used to argue that no single lab will be the only company left.

Also seen as weak appropriability, complements capture value

How the sources use it

  • Vinoo Ganesh (Latent Space, 2026-09-12) his moat is verified operational knowledge, since the model is rented and cheapening.
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-09-11) Catalini's economics of open versus closed. Cross-source (Berman); new to this source.
  • Christian Catalini (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-19) the scarce resources (compute, data, distribution) that capture a general-purpose technology's value instead of its original inventors.

Token share versus revenue share
