Reasoning tokens

first seen 2026-W34 · 6 links · 1 min read

The internal deliberation tokens a model spends before answering; Tunguz quantifies how a smaller model spends more of them to close the gap with a larger one.

Also seen as thinking tokens, deliberation tokens

How the sources use it

  • Jaejun Shim, HyunJin Kim, Young Jin Kim et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Technically); the quantity the method reduces on easy instances.
  • Xinxin Song, Siyuan Li, Tingxiong Xiao et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz); the advantage is allocated per token according to visual support.
  • Keertana Chidambaram, Andrew Ilyas, Vasilis Syrgkanis (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-14) cross-source; monitor reasoning budget is shown to be spent rationalising the injection.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-09-01) reasoning variants as a dial to turn off for casual use.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-18) the internal deliberation tokens a smaller model spends to close the knowledge gap with a larger one.

Related terms

concept

Inference-time compute

Spending more computation at answer time (longer reasoning, more candidates, verification) rather than in training; OpenAI's strategic bet in Lambert's reading and the cost the arXiv verification papers try to reduce.

needed by 3 · 13 linksconcept

Reasoning effort levels

The effort dial (low to max) that sets how much a model thinks per request, with a 33x cost range on one prompt in Willison's test and two settings apparently identical.

7 linksmetric

Token efficiency

Capability per token consumed or produced: the gap between intelligence rank and verbosity rank, the 30-to-1 input-to-output ratio of agentic work, capability per training token in A.X K2.

9 linksconcept

Reasoning traces

Records of a model's step-by-step reasoning; as training data they are the asset Meta buys cheaply through prompt pricing, as oversight they are what monitors read, and as evidence they are what Willison inspects per effort level.

needed by 2 · 9 linkstechnique

Recurrent depth

A looped-transformer technique, reported by The Information to be used in GPT-6 Astra, that repeats computation through the same layers so that part of a model's reasoning happens inside the network rather than in its written chain of thought.
