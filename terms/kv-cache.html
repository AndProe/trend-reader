KV cache

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 2 · 12 links · 2 min read

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

Also seen as KV-cache, KV cache capacity, KV cache reuse, key-value cache

How the sources use it

  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-21) the constraint he wants coding agents freed from; appending to one cache is what locks an agent to one model and blocks state management. Recurring and cross-source (SemiAnalysis, arXiv cs.AI).
  • Dylan Patel, Tanj Bennett, Bryan Shan (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-21) treated as an immutable, forward-only blob store rather than state attached to a machine, and as the term that ends pipeline depth. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Cam Quilici, Alec Ibarra, Kimbo Chen, Myron Xie (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-18) offloading the table frees HBM for weights and KV cache, allowing larger batches or more sessions. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Mingyang Mao, Wyatt Mackey, Xiaomin Lin (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Matthew Berman); the paper treats the cache as mutable state that needs repair, not only as memory to place.
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Wenyao Gao (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-14) prefix reuse served from cache as the defining trait of agentic sessions. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Harrison Barclay, Minjae Kang (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-13) per-user cache as the capacity that taller stacks buy, and the budget cut in the InferenceX experiment. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-11) the memory component DeepSeek cut to a quarter. Cross-source, first use in Berman notes.
  • Arya Tschand, Yaosheng Fu, Vikram Sharma Mailthody et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-11) cross-source; top-k selection must index the full KV, which drives the split.
  • Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI, 2026-09-09) the cost looping does not remove, and Nanbeige's failed cache-sharing experiment.
  • Dylan Patel, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Bryan Shan, Wenyao Gao, Daniel Nishball, Zane Fong (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-07) layout, offloading and hybrid prefix caching as the bulk of the optimisation work.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-24) the systems problem agentic serving turns on.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-19) the long-context constraint.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-17) the standard transformer memory structure that grows linearly with context, which test-time training replaces with a fixed-size weight update.

Related terms

concept

Long context

Context windows of hundreds of thousands to a million tokens and what they enable and cost: multi-turn agent sessions, memory-bound serving, degraded reliability at the far end.

needed by 4 · 12 linkstechnique

Prefill-decode disaggregation

Serving prompt processing and token generation on separate hardware pools with KV cache transferred between them; behind Nvidia's best agentic results and absent by design from OpenAI's Jalapeño.

6 linksconcept

Agentic inference workloads

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns.

needed by 1 · 16 linkstechnique

Sparse attention

Attention mechanisms that attend to a subset of positions so that long contexts stay tractable; A.X K2's long-context mechanism.

6 linksmodel

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

DeepSeek's open-weights release, which Berman reads as benchmarking level with Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol and as his evidence that open weights reach the previous frontier generation about six months behind it.

5 linkstechnique

Test-time training

A model updating its weights on the prompt it is answering rather than staying frozen, replacing the linearly growing KV cache with a fixed-size update; Tunguz's 'When Models Learn'.
