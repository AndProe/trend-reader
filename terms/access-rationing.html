Access rationing

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 1 · 15 links · 1 min read

Labs and governments restricting who may use the strongest models: withdrawing models from distrusted platforms, gating frontier tiers, nationality screening and trusted-partner programmes. Tunguz's term for the pattern; the Cursor cut-off and Project Glasswing are its two reference cases.

Also seen as model access cut-off, whitelists and blacklists, frontier AI lab wars, frontier segmentation

How the sources use it

  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-22) Zvi's rebuttal to Vance on defenders being denied models: the administration took Fable 5 off the market and picks who may use Mythos. Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Matthew Berman).
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-02) capability withheld from general release and allocated by category. Cross-source (Tunguz, SemiAnalysis, Berman).
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-01) labs withdrawing models from platforms they distrust; the same event Tunguz and Orosz cover.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-01) Tunguz's term; SemiAnalysis extends it to the prediction that API access to the strongest models may end.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-31) (aliases: whitelists and blacklists) — labs deciding who may use their strongest models rather than selling to all comers by the token.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-18) the US-China AI alliance split as a geopolitical version of the model-access rationing pattern seen elsewhere this month.

Related terms

concept

Frontier models

The strongest available models at any moment, now gated rather than openly sold and bypassed by most real usage; the tier the open models chase and the reference point for every pricing and access argument in the vault.

needed by 7 · 21 linksconcept

Trusted-access programs

Restricted distribution of a lab's strongest model or capabilities to vetted partners (Project Glasswing, Fair Wind, Daybreak); Berman's term for what Tunguz calls access rationing from the supply side.

needed by 1 · 10 linksconcept

Frontier model access

Which governments and allies get to use the strongest models, treated as a national-security instrument (Five Eyes access) and, in SemiAnalysis's Korea post, as an exposure: bans and delays show that API access to frontier models is not a stable foundation.

8 linksconcept

Export controls

Government restrictions on which chips and models may leave the country and who may run them, including nationality screening for frontier inference and criminal cases over smuggled AI servers.

7 linkstechnique

Distillation attacks

Training a model on a rival's outputs to copy its capability; the fear behind access cut-offs, Anthropic's countermeasures in Fable 5.1, and Zuckerberg's call to legalise it domestically.

9 linksevent

Cursor-xAI acquisition

The April 2026 deal of roughly $60 billion in which SpaceX and xAI bought Cursor for its coding data and idle GPU capacity, which Berman credits for Grok 4.6 and which triggered OpenAI's model cut-off.
