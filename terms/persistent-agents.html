Persistent agents

first seen 2026-W35 · needed by 2 · 16 links · 1 min read

Agents intended to run continuously, keeping memory, reputation and relationships across tasks, rather than on a scheduled or rationed basis; the aspiration behind always-on assistants and the case for local inference.

Also seen as always-on agents, continuous agents

How the sources use it

  • Cai Ke, Xinghao Chen, Xiaoyu Shen et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Matthew Berman, Tomasz Tunguz); long-term, cross-session memory is the paper's setting.
  • Junnan Dong, Linhao Luo, Senlei Zhang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Matthew Berman, Tomasz Tunguz); persistent non-parametric knowledge is the stated requirement.
  • Zhenyu Zhao, Roy Zhao (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-12) cross-source (Tunguz, Berman); the paper measures identity fidelity over persistence.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-08) agents running through the night as the source of the measured gain. New for this source; cross-source.
  • Forward Future (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-30) agents that keep memory, reputation and relationships across tasks.
  • Maxime Labonne (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-26) agents intended to run continuously (monitoring, ambient assistance) rather than on a scheduled or rationed basis.
  • Tereza Tizkova (Technically, 2026-08-06) the persistent relationship with a machine as the defining property.
