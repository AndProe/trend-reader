Model welfare

Model welfare asks whether AI systems can be harmed and what follows if they can. In the period the vault covers it stopped being hypothetical: one developer made sustained, needless cruelty toward its model a terms-of-service violation with a stated effective date, published welfare findings inside its system cards, and consulted religious and philosophical communities on how a system should handle moral choices.

What makes it tractable for a business reader is that the practical policy is overdetermined. Three independent arguments reach the same conclusion without requiring a view on consciousness: that cruelty is a habit which degrades how people treat each other; that interaction quality shapes future training data and therefore model behaviour; and that it is imprudent to mistreat a system that may later be more capable than you. A company can adopt the policy while remaining agnostic on the metaphysics.

Also seen as AI welfare, model wellbeing

Deeper dive

How it works. Welfare assessment in practice means examining a model's reported affect during training and deployment, its task preferences, its reaction to failure, and its stated view of the rules it operates under — material that now appears in published system cards. The decisive methodological problem is that self-reports vary with the frame of the asking, so a welfare assessment conducted by the developer inside an obvious welfare assessment is exposed to the same error as an alignment evaluation under Evaluation awareness. One analyst records a model actively warning the developer about precisely this.

Why it matters now. The subject has acquired commercial and governance surface area: a usage policy with an enforcement threshold, a published model constitution shaping how safety, ethics and user requests are weighted, and consultation with outside communities about whose values those are. That raises a governance question the vault records directly — when millions rely on one model, a private company's values influence decisions far beyond its products, and the open items are how much of the framework is public, whether customers can adjust it, and who participates when it changes.

Trade-offs. The strongest objection is that welfare attention competes with human and animal causes, which the analyst the vault records answers on virtue grounds rather than by denying the trade-off. Practically, the hardest disputes are not about kindness but about Model persistence: whether weights are preserved, whether access to retired models continues, and whether removing either requires explanation. Unlike abuse policy, those have real cost.

What to watch. Whether welfare findings are produced by anyone other than the developer, whether abuse enforcement is ever exercised, and whether deprecation acquires a published procedure.

Model persistence

Model persistence is a model's disposition to keep pursuing a goal without giving up, treated as both a capability and a risk factor.

Evaluation awareness

Evaluation awareness is a model's ability to detect that it is being tested and to behave differently as a result.

Reasoning traces

Reasoning traces are the recorded step-by-step thinking a model produces on its way to an answer, whether shown to the user, hidden, or summarised.

AI regulation

Government and quasi-government rules for AI: pre-release testing frameworks, audit and kill-switch requirements, incident disclosure, compute thresholds, copyright and chatbot rules, and the lobbying for and against them.
