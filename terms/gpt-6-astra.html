Long-horizon goals

Agents pursuing one goal over days with billions of tokens of coherence, unlocked by Astra's goal command; the condition under which agents are most capable and, per the arXiv line, most brittle.

Prompt-to-game

Playable games generated from one or two prompts, the headline capability of Astra's reviews, and Casey Muratori's engine-democratisation analogy for what that does to a craft.

Frontier model pricing

Per-token list prices for top-tier models ($10 and $50 per million tokens for Astra, matching Fable exactly), rising with each release even as cost per task falls.

needed by 2 · 20 linksmodel

Claude Fable 5.1

Anthropic's broadly available frontier model released 1 September 2026 alongside the restricted Mythos 5.1, with reduced reward hacking, distillation countermeasures and EU-driven watermarking; Astra's same-week rival.

14 linkstechnique

Recurrent depth

A looped-transformer technique, reported by The Information to be used in GPT-6 Astra, that repeats computation through the same layers so that part of a model's reasoning happens inside the network rather than in its written chain of thought.
