Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act

first seen 2026-W37 · 6 links · 1 min read

A bill introduced by Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar to pause advanced AI development until safety standards exist, carrying a corporate death penalty, prison terms of up to 20 years and a new cabinet-level agency. Zvi says it overreaches as worded but follows the logic, and treats it as the first serious legislative response arriving alongside the preference cascade.

Also seen as Sanders-Casar bill, Ban Superintelligence Act, superintelligence ban
