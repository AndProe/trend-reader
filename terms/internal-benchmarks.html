Internal benchmarks

first seen 2026-W33 · needed by 1 · 12 links · 1 min read

Evals a company writes for its own workloads, the tests for non-deterministic behaviour, expected to merge with telemetry; Berman's standing advice against trusting vendor tables.

Also seen as evals, private evals, company benchmarks, Box AI evals

Related terms

Observability

Telemetry as a product decision: wide events replacing logs and metrics, traces of traces for hours-long agent runs, auto-instrumentation now easier than not.

needed by 2 · 8 linkstechnique

Deployment qualification

Statistically qualifying an agent together with its oversight policy against a reliability target before deployment (READY), or a constrained-success metric that counts only acceptable completions; the answer to benchmarks that do not predict production.

10 linkstechnique

LLM-as-judge

Using a model or a panel of models to score outputs against a rubric, from Tunguz's writing-quality experiment to WorldBench's scoring.

4 linkstechnique

Verification loops

Pipelines that retry and validate each change until it passes tests, the common ingredient in every AI-migration success story and the basis of software factories.

needed by 3 · 14 linksproduct

SWE-bench Verified

The human-validated subset of SWE-bench, a repository-repair benchmark on which a coding agent must resolve real GitHub issues, used as the standard test bed for coding-agent research.

14 linksconcept

Causal grader

A scorer that checks how an answer was reached and rejects one obtained by an illegitimate route, as opposed to an acausal grader that accepts any answer matching the key.
