Hyperscalers

first seen 2026-W13 · needed by 1 · 16 links · 2 min read

The largest cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, plus Oracle and increasingly the labs themselves) that buy most accelerators, issue most AI debt and grow 37 to 82 percent a year; their decelerating share of Nvidia's growth is a tracked signal.

Also seen as cloud providers, clouds, hyperscaler cloud growth rates

Related terms

concept

AI infrastructure debt

The borrowing needed to build AI data centres, estimated by Tunguz at about $4 trillion over five years, and the instruments carrying it: project finance at 65 to 75 percent leverage, off-balance-sheet vehicles, private credit, commercial paper, corporate and municipal bonds.

needed by 1 · 9 linksconcept

GPU access

Who can get frontier compute and on what terms: hyperscalers locking up the pipeline, scarcity for everyone outside it, surplus in one company meeting shortage in another.

needed by 1 · 8 linksconcept

Edge AI

Running inference on local hardware (Macs, single GPUs, devices) rather than in a data centre, driven by cheap capable open models; Tunguz's mainframe-to-personal analogy.

9 linksconcept

Vendor financing

Nvidia extending its balance sheet to sustain demand for its own chips: investing in labs and neoclouds, financing data centres directly, financing SpaceX's buildout.

9 linksconcept

Object storage

Cloud storage for files and unstructured data as a bottomless, cheap, separately billed service (AWS S3 since 2006); the layer on which Snowflake, Databricks, Neon and Turbopuffer rebuilt data infrastructure by separating storage from compute, and where a vibe-coded app keeps its images and PDFs while the database keeps only the link.

4 linksconcept

Grid interconnection

Connecting a data centre or power plant to the utility grid through the queue a grid operator runs, which SemiAnalysis's energy team treats as the constraint behind-the-meter power routes around.
