Agentic inference workloads

first seen 2026-W35 · needed by 1 · 16 links · 2 min read

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns. SemiAnalysis argues this, not single-turn chat, now dominates inference and should be what hardware is benchmarked on.

Also seen as agentic traffic, agent serving, multi-turn long-context serving

Related terms

concept

AI agents

Software systems in which a language model plans, calls tools and acts over many steps toward a goal, rather than answering a single prompt.

needed by 20 · 33 linksconcept

Long context

Context windows of hundreds of thousands to a million tokens and what they enable and cost: multi-turn agent sessions, memory-bound serving, degraded reliability at the far end.

needed by 4 · 12 linkstechnique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

needed by 2 · 12 linkstechnique

Prefill-decode disaggregation

Serving prompt processing and token generation on separate hardware pools with KV cache transferred between them; behind Nvidia's best agentic results and absent by design from OpenAI's Jalapeño.

6 linkstechnique

Sub-agents

Parallel agents spawned inside a run under a coordinator, now too many to read and the source of bursty serving traffic; dozens with bounded concurrency in Latent Space's Astra practice.

needed by 1 · 13 linksmetric

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.
