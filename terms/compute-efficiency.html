Compute efficiency

first seen 2026-W34 · 6 links · 1 min read

Capability delivered per unit of compute, combining model-side gains (active parameters, cost reductions) and chip-side gains; Tunguz's second profit lever alongside pricing.

Also seen as model efficiency gains

How the sources use it

  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-09-19) RSI as a route to making models cheaper at a fixed intelligence level. Cross-source; new to this source.
  • Wonmi Choi, Minuk Park, Zhixiong Niu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Dwarkesh Patel); the authors' framing is wasted resources under concurrency.
  • Mingyang Mao, Wyatt Mackey, Xiaomin Lin (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz); 13 to 21 times less recomputation for a 0.94 margin recovery.
  • Junnan Dong, Linhao Luo, Senlei Zhang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz); the GPU-to-GPU exchange protocol is offered as the reason for the 10.5 times training speed-up.
  • Dwarkesh Patel (Dwarkesh Patel, 2026-09-08) measured here as compute multipliers split into model and data sides. Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Tunguz).
  • FuneralAI 葬AI (ChinaTalk, 2026-09-06) his manufacturing analogy, in which a fully usable small model beats Fable 5 on efficiency. Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Dwarkesh Patel).

  • Jordan Schneider (ChinaTalk, 2026-09-02) his claim that attacker capex is low because efficiency keeps improving; a bedroom NVIDIA rig running a near-frontier model. Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Dwarkesh Patel).

  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-21) the combined model- and chip-driven efficiency gain.

Related terms

Performance per watt

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

11 linksmetric

Token efficiency

Capability per token consumed or produced: the gap between intelligence rank and verbosity rank, the 30-to-1 input-to-output ratio of agentic work, capability per training token in A.X K2.

9 linkstechnique

Mixture of experts

An architecture that activates only a fraction of parameters per token (GLM 5.3 Flash: 320B total, 18B active), the mechanism behind cheap capable open models.

7 linksmetric

Revenue per gigawatt

Revenue earned per unit of power capacity: Tunguz's $50 million per megawatt against $10 to 15 million of cost, SemiAnalysis's $100 billion-plus per gigawatt-year for frontier APIs, Nvidia's metric for owning more of each AI factory.
