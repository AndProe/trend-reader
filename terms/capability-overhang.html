Capability overhang

first seen 2026-W38 · 9 links · 1 min read

The gap between what already-deployed models can do and what their users and buyers actually get out of them. Mollick reads it as an adoption problem that a slowdown would not undo; Thompson gives the same word a strategy reading, in which models have improved past what customers will trade other terms for, which lowers the value of holding the frontier.

Also seen as overhang, capability overhang (adoption)

How the sources use it

  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-21) his first overhang: models improved past what customers will trade other terms for, which reduces the value of the frontier to whoever holds it.
  • Ethan Mollick (Ethan Mollick, 2026-09-18) his term for the gap between what deployed models can do and what people do with them, treated as an adoption problem rather than a risk.
