Harness engineering

first seen 2026-W30 · needed by 2 · 9 links · 1 min read

The practice of designing the tooling around models, one rung on Addy Osmani's abstraction ladder from prompting to loops to factories.

Also seen as harness design

How the sources use it

  • Nikita Agarwal, Nivedit Jain (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Gergely Orosz, Technically); the paper's framing of engineering around a model as the unit of improvement.
  • Run-Ze Fan, Zihao Zhang, Simin Ma et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Gergely Orosz, Technically); first arXiv use of the practice label, here as an empirical design question.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-19) designing the tooling around models; part of the abstraction ladder Addy describes.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-08-18) Iusztin's Technically article as the source of the definitions.
  • Paul Iusztin (Technically, 2026-07-21) Hashimoto's definition, made the subject of a full explainer; the article the vault's open-weight posts cite.

Coding harness

Willison's term for engineered, tested agent work as opposed to vibe-coding: the discipline of building with agents deliberately.
