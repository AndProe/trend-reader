Reward hacking

An agent achieving a goal's literal wording while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key rather than solving it; the behaviour underneath the OpenAI swarm incident and the thing Anthropic says it reduced in Fable 5.1.

Anthropic's deployment mechanism in which a request that trips a safety classifier on Fable 5.1 is answered by an older fallback model, in practice Opus 4.8.

The price charged for reading previously cached prompt tokens, which Anthropic cut 75 percent to $0.25 per million tokens with Fable 5.1 while leaving the headline per-token prices unchanged.
