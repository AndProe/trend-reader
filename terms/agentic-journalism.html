Agentic journalism

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 1 min read

News production where agents monitor, research and draft under human-authored rules and a human keeps final judgement on a subset of stories; the one-person newsroom operating at former-masthead scale on machine-readable sources.

Also seen as one-person newsroom, machine-readable news surface

How the sources use it

  • Alex Salkever (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-25) news production where agents handle monitoring, research, drafting and much of editing under human-authored rules, with a human retaining final publish judgment on only a subset of stories.
  • Alex Salkever (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-25) the broader phenomenon of a single individual operating at a scale previously requiring a full masthead, paralleling the "one-person billion-dollar company" trope in software.
  • Alex Salkever (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-25) Salkever's term for the growing share of source material (X posts, filings, GitHub releases, arXiv papers) that is inherently machine-parseable rather than requiring human legwork.
