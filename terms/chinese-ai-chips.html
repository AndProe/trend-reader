Chinese AI chips

first seen 2026-W31 · 8 links · 1 min read

Domestically designed accelerators (Ascend and successors) and the co-designed chip, interconnect and serving stacks that now train and serve frontier-adjacent Chinese models at scale, shown by GLM 5.3 Flash serving 100 trillion tokens a day and LongCat trained end to end on Ascend.

Also seen as Chinese accelerators, Ascend, domestic Chinese silicon

Related terms

concept

Custom silicon

Accelerators designed by the companies that use them rather than bought from Nvidia: Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA, OpenAI's Jalapeño, Cerebras wafers for fast tiers.

needed by 2 · 9 linksconcept

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.

needed by 11 · 36 linksmodel

GLM 5.3 Flash

Z.ai's efficient open-weights model (320B total, 18B active), first seen anonymously as Ox-Alpha on OpenRouter, then confirmed serving 100 trillion tokens a day on Chinese chips; Berman's 'cancel your subscriptions' release.

4 linksconcept

Sovereign AI models

Frontier-scale models trained by or for a state in its own language and under its own control (A.X K2, Korea's tournament models), motivated by the exposure of depending on American frontier APIs.

10 linksconcept

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.

15 linksconcept

CUDA moat

Nvidia's software advantage: the CUDA stack and the day-0 framework support that make its GPUs the default target for every serving engine, which competitors must match before their hardware counts.
