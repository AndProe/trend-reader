Release cadence

first seen 2026-W33 · 9 links · 1 min read

How often labs ship models, treated by Lambert as the main competitive lever for Chinese labs (days versus months) and quantified by SemiAnalysis at one frontier release every 51 days in the agentic era.

Also seen as time to release, pre-release testing window, shipping cadence

How the sources use it

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-08) four labs shipping updates in one week and the resulting model fatigue. Cross-source, first use in Berman notes.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-03) a third generation that is five times sharper than the second, one year on.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-02) three Flash releases in six weeks stated by the lab itself. Cross-source (Interconnects, SemiAnalysis).
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) a point release marketed as "production-ready" one generation after launch. Cross-source (Interconnects, SemiAnalysis).
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-21) Interconnects' term; here quantified for the frontier duopoly.
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-08-14) days versus months as the main competitive lever.

Open-closed capability gap

Distillation attacks
