RLCD

first seen 2026-W38 · 10 links · 1 min read

Training a model to answer many typed questions about one input with calibrated probabilities in a single pass, rather than to generate text — the method behind Jev, which TypeSafe presents as a replacement for RLHF for decision work. Guo and colleagues report that the calibration transfers: a single generic question reaches a median AUROC of 0.886 zero-shot across ten alignment-failure types at 63 times lower cost than an LLM judge, while a visual port finds accuracy gains that do not carry the calibration with them.

Also seen as reinforcement learning for calibrated decisions, reinforcement learning from calibrated decisions
