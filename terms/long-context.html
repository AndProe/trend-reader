Long context

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 4 · 12 links · 1 min read

Context windows of hundreds of thousands to a million tokens and what they enable and cost: multi-turn agent sessions, memory-bound serving, degraded reliability at the far end.

Also seen as 1M context, long context window

How the sources use it

  • Trang Nguyen, Eulrang Cho, Bingqing Chen, Tim Dettmers (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Simon Willison, Technically); the paper treats the bounded window as the binding constraint on multi-session work.
  • Dhruv Srikanth, Bingchen Zhao, Dixing Xu, Yuxiang Wu, Zhengyao Jiang (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Simon Willison, Technically); among the seven discovered improvements are memory mechanisms for the agent's growing context.
  • Dylan Patel, Tanj Bennett, Bryan Shan (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-21) the 500k-token agent prefill and the million-token compaction ceiling are what make the KV read dominate decode. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Run-Ze Fan, Zihao Zhang, Simin Ma et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source; context-window budgets are one of the two axes varied.
  • Mingyang Mao, Wyatt Mackey, Xiaomin Lin (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source; the cost of re-prefill scales with the context the cache holds.
  • Cai Ke, Xinghao Chen, Xiaoyu Shen et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source; the history is too long and noisy to reason over directly, which is the motivation for the latent graph.
  • Junnan Dong, Linhao Luo, Senlei Zhang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source; dense document contexts are what the representation is built to carry.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-09-01) the context-window dial and its memory cost.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-01) multi-hour video is the setting; the loop replaces stuffing frames into context.
  • Cheolseung Baek (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-08-31) 256K evaluated. Existing concept.
  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Wega Chu, Jordan Nanos (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-19) 44 GB of SRAM per wafer as the long-context constraint the KV cache runs into.

Related terms

technique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

needed by 2 · 12 linksconcept

Context rot

The degradation of an agent's performance and memory reliability as its conversation history accumulates; Tunguz's reason to ask how long an agent should live.

9 linkstechnique

Sparse attention

Attention mechanisms that attend to a subset of positions so that long contexts stay tractable; A.X K2's long-context mechanism.

6 linksconcept

Agentic inference workloads

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns.

needed by 1 · 16 linksconcept

Long-horizon goals

Agents pursuing one goal over days with billions of tokens of coherence, unlocked by Astra's goal command; the condition under which agents are most capable and, per the arXiv line, most brittle.

needed by 1 · 12 linksconcept

Vision-language models

Models that take images and text together and reason over both, the model class behind computer-use agents that read screens and behind visually grounded reasoning research.
