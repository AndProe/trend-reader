Nemotron

first seen 2026-W33 · 3 links · 1 min read

Nvidia's open-source model family from single-GPU Lightning to a trillion-plus flagship, backed by a reported $26 billion programme; the instrument of Nvidia's commoditise-the-complement strategy.

Also seen as Nemotron 3, NVIDIA Nemotron
