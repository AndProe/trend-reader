LLM-as-judge

first seen 2026-W36 · 4 links · 1 min read

Using a model or a panel of models to score outputs against a rubric, from Tunguz's writing-quality experiment to WorldBench's scoring.

Also seen as model-as-judge, AI grader

How the sources use it

  • Haoyuan Ma, Hengwei Liu, Linjuan Wu, Yongliang Shen, Weiming Lu (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Tomasz Tunguz); used as the quality scorer inside the selection pipeline rather than as an evaluator of outputs.
  • Abbas Raza Ali, Muhammad Ajmal Siddiqui, Moona Zahid (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-18) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Tomasz Tunguz); used as a calibrated scaler for blinded expert judgement rather than as the grader of record.
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-17) Claude judging automation levels and classifying compute, with the lab noting the shared-error problem. Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, arXiv cs.AI); new to this source.
  • Zhenyu Zhao, Roy Zhao (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-12) cross-source; the 12.5-point gap on replayed responses is presented as judge sensitivity, not target behaviour.
  • Shrey Nag, Sachita, Abhishek Kumar Singh et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-09) cross-source; the single fixed judge is one of the evaluated models, flagged as a limitation.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-02) an AI panel scoring writing quality against a rubric; the evaluation method used.
  • Leonardo Ranaldi (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-01) used inside the scoring. Existing vault term.
