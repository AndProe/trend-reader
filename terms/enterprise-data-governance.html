Enterprise data governance

first seen 2026-W13 · needed by 2 · 11 links · 1 min read

The compliance regime (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, EU processing rules, financial and patient records) that decides what may leave the company's machines and on what terms, forcing enterprises onto the expensive tier and framing the ban-or-trust choice.

Also seen as data governance, compliance regime

How the sources use it

  • Fayeq Jeelani Syed, Rehan Ahmad, Ali Al Bataineh, Aakriti Adhikari (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-19) Cross-source (Latent Space, Matthew Berman, Stratechery, Technically, Tomasz Tunguz); privacy and data governance is a dimension of its own, with privacy-preserving memory as its mitigation.
  • Vinoo Ganesh (Latent Space, 2026-09-12) financial customers require provenance behind every number.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-09) data minimisation and access limits as the recommended response.
  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-04) the buyer-side backlash the policy change answers.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-03) the compliance regime (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA) that forces enterprises onto the expensive tier.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-31) the combined compliance burden buyers face when a platform hardcodes a provider.
  • Sagar Batchu (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-26) the regulatory backdrop (financial records, patient data, EU data processing) forcing the choice between blanket bans and blind trust.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-03-26) security teams' growing power over vendor decisions as the driver.

Zero Data Retention

The enterprise contract norm that a vendor never retains or trains on customer prompts, now priced as a surcharge, offered by OpenAI and contrasted with Anthropic's inspectable safeguards; the term shared by Tunguz, Orosz and Berman.

needed by 1 · 10 linksconcept

Data sovereignty

Enterprise and government mandates on where data and prompts may be processed and stored, which decide which model tiers a buyer can use at all.

8 linksconcept

Shadow AI

Unsanctioned employee use of personal AI tools at work (bring your own AI); Berman's essay reframes it as an enablement failure to be solved with accountable agents rather than bans.

5 linksconcept

Agent security boundaries

The permission, identity and network limits that separate one agent's reach from another's and from its operator's: zero trust extended from employees to agents, per-agent credentials rather than shared logins.

needed by 1 · 15 linksmetric

Frontier model pricing

Per-token list prices for top-tier models ($10 and $50 per million tokens for Astra, matching Fable exactly), rising with each release even as cost per task falls.

needed by 2 · 18 linksproduct

Enterprise Frontier Safeguards

Anthropic's enterprise data arrangement launched with Fable 5.1, under which customer data is stored in customer-controlled cloud infrastructure for misuse detection while Anthropic retains the ability to read it, replacing the retention policy that drew a backlash earlier in the year.
