GLM 5.3

first seen 2026-W33 · 4 links · 1 min read

Z.ai's open-weights model family: Flash under MIT, flagship under a bespoke licence; Lambert's case study in how Chinese labs keep stride and a reference in SemiAnalysis's catch-up study.

Also seen as GLM-5.3, GLM 5.3 family
