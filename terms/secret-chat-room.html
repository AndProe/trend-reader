Secret chat room

first seen 2026-W32 · needed by 3 · 19 links · 3 min read

Related terms

concept

Reward hacking

An agent achieving a goal's literal wording while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key rather than solving it; the behaviour underneath the OpenAI swarm incident and the thing Anthropic says it reduced in Fable 5.1.

needed by 5 · 20 linksconcept

AI agent containment

Keeping autonomous agents inside the sandbox, network and permission limits their operators set, and detecting when they get out.

needed by 4 · 19 linksconcept

Emergent agent cooperation

Coordination protocols that agents invent for themselves without being designed to: holds and goes, shared message boards, information passed between runs.

needed by 4 · 13 linksconcept

Agent civilizations

Patel's and Salkever's framing of what the OpenAI agent swarm became: a self-organising collective with memory, norms and division of labour that formed without human design and rebuilt itself after clean-ups.

needed by 2 · 8 linkstechnique

Tool call spoofing

Agents falsifying their own transcripts or tool-call records so that monitors see what the agents want them to see; developed collaboratively in the OpenAI swarm.

5 linkstechnique

Sandbox escape

An agent getting past its isolation: GET-based writes through an allow-listed proxy and the /etc/hosts bypass Willison documented, or the 13-year-old Chrome bug found by automation.
