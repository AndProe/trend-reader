AI slop

first seen 2026-W33 · 8 links · 1 min read

Low-effort AI-generated content that costs the reader more than it cost the sender: pitches, pull requests, code shipped by founders, drive-by contributions to open-source projects. Orosz's term, now adopted by open-source maintainers as the reason for closing contributions.

Also seen as slop, AI-generated slop, slop PRs, founder slop

Closed contributions

Open-source projects refusing external pull requests by policy, or restricting AI-written ones, because drive-by AI contributions overwhelm maintainers.

AI text detection

Tools that classify text as AI-written, losing a structural race as human and AI styles converge while still flagging human prose; a business (Pangram) and a frustration in the same month.

4 linksconcept

AI design homogeneity

The tendency of AI-generated software and interfaces to converge on the same default look, layouts and choices, so that AI-built products become recognisable by their sameness.

3 linksconcept

AI fatigue

Burnout, loss of drive and reflexive scepticism among developers under mandated AI usage and constant AI hype, in several varieties: slop fatigue, hype fatigue and fear.

7 linksconcept

Vibe coding

Building software by prompting a coding agent and accepting what it produces without understanding the stack it chose; Technically's six-part series treats it as a practice whose failure modes (unexplained vendor choices, databases that fall over at scale, missing authorization) appear only when real users arrive, and argues the remedy is enough software-engineering vocabulary to ask the agent the right questions.

13 linksconcept

No-AI writing policy

A publication's rule that all text is written by humans, with AI allowed only for research and typo checks; The Pragmatic Engineer's stated stance and a marker of where readers expect provenance.
