Long-horizon goals

first seen 2026-W36 · needed by 1 · 12 links · 1 min read

Agents pursuing one goal over days with billions of tokens of coherence, unlocked by Astra's goal command; the condition under which agents are most capable and, per the arXiv line, most brittle.

Also seen as goal runs, multi-day runs, /goal runs

How the sources use it

  • METR (METR, 2026-09-22) the difficult long-horizon tasks on which METR records qualitative weaknesses. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Wenbo Pan, Zhichao Liu, Shujie Liu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Gergely Orosz, Latent Space, Matthew Berman); the paper localises the difficulty to specific decision points inside the horizon rather than to its length.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-15) the /goal command and threads running for days as the driver of adoption. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Zishuo Zhao, Kai Chen, Ao Li et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-15) cross-source; multi-turn tasks are where the turn-weighting is claimed to matter.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-09) /goal as a crutch newer models no longer need. New for this source;.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-09) an 88-hour multi-agent run on a single problem.
  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-03) Berman's term; billions of tokens of coherence here.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-03) multi-day autonomous runs via a goal command; the behaviour that unlocks ambitious output.
  • Leonardo Ranaldi (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-01) Berman's term for multi-day agent runs; here the condition under which agents are most brittle.
