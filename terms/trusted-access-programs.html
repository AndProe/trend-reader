Trusted-access programs

first seen 2026-W35 · needed by 1 · 10 links · 1 min read

Restricted distribution of a lab's strongest model or capabilities to vetted partners (Project Glasswing, Fair Wind, Daybreak); Berman's term for what Tunguz calls access rationing from the supply side.

Also seen as trusted access, Fair Wind, Daybreak, trusted partners

How the sources use it

  • METR (METR, 2026-09-22) 10 business days of API access, a questionnaire, a researcher interview, and a second METR team with elevated access whose evidence the authors could not see. Recurring (METR, W36); cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Berman, Tunguz).
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-10) the lab's stated conclusion for frontier biology capability. Cross-source (Berman).
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-02) restricted distribution of the strongest model; the same pattern Tunguz calls access rationing.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-02) the Fairwind Program is the vault's "Fair Wind" alias, now with the lab's own description. Cross-source (Berman).
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-02) the programme is the term's "Fair Wind" alias in the lab's own words: vetted partners, staged categories, usage conditions. Cross-source (Berman).
  • METR (METR, 2026-08-31) METR's nonpublic model access and keys for models without production safeguards as the assets being defended. Cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Berman, Tunguz).
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) a waitlisted research preview for selected labs and manufacturers before open release. Cross-source (Berman).
