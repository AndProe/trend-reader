Deployment qualification

first seen 2026-W35 · 10 links · 1 min read

Statistically qualifying an agent together with its oversight policy against a reliability target before deployment (READY), or a constrained-success metric that counts only acceptable completions; the answer to benchmarks that do not predict production.

Also seen as READY, reliability qualification, constrained task success

How the sources use it

  • METR (METR, 2026-09-22) a predeployment evaluation feeding a system card, with METR stating it does not verify any policy threshold. New for this source; cross-source (arXiv).
  • Jennifer Williams, Dave Farris, Jeff Farris, Jiantao Jiao (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Recurring (arXiv cs.AI); the end-to-end serving tests and performance gates function as a production-correctness gate on top of unit passing.
  • Jack Clark (Import AI, 2026-09-21) RAND's Preparedness archetype: phased deployment, independent evaluation and incident reporting as voluntary commitments. Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI); new to this source.
  • Andrew Anogie Uduimoh, Hadiza Umar Yusuf, Oluwafemi Osho (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Recurring (arXiv cs.AI); pre-deployment evaluation before procurement is the paper's unit, framed as a regulatory requirement rather than a vendor practice.
  • Abbas Raza Ali, Muhammad Ajmal Siddiqui, Moona Zahid (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-18) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Import AI, METR); the two-tier gate is stated as an executable algorithm returning REJECT, CONDITIONAL or SCALE.
  • Yanzhang Ma, Zhenghan Tai, Hanwei Wu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) recurring (arXiv: READY); a patch must earn deployment, and only 6 of 33 did.
  • Veronica Chatrath (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-02) statistically qualifying an agent plus oversight policy against a reliability target.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) a point release marketed as production-ready, the lab's own bar for when a model is fit for developers to build on.
