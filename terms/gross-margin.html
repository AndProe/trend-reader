Gross margin

first seen 2026-W32 · 5 links · 1 min read

Revenue minus cost of serving: Nvidia at 75 percent, Anthropic from minus 94 percent to 40 to 50 percent, SemiAnalysis's 60 to 85 percent inference-margin estimates. The number that says whether tokens are a business.

Also seen as inference gross margins, serving margin
