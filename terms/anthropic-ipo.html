Anthropic IPO

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 1 min read

Anthropic's move toward a public listing, reported from August 2026 as a potentially record-matching offering and used across Berman's daily issues as the frame for the lab's compute commitments and its profitability claims. Berman's own question is what it means to list a company whose leadership calls its product an existential risk.
