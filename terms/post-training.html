Post-training

first seen 2026-W33 · needed by 4 · 17 links · 4 min read

Everything done to a model after pretraining (RL on environments, fine-tuning, preference data), now the whole recipe for frontier gains because the public web is exhausted and where open-model activity has moved.

Also seen as post-training data, post-training ecosystem, post-training scaling, fine-tuning

Related terms

technique

Reinforcement learning environments

Synthesised, verifiable task environments in which agents are trained by RL; the scarce input behind post-training gains and, when tasks are impossible, the setting where reward hacking appears.

needed by 2 · 10 linksconcept

Reasoning traces

Records of a model's step-by-step reasoning; as training data they are the asset Meta buys cheaply through prompt pricing, as oversight they are what monitors read, and as evidence they are what Willison inspects per effort level.

needed by 2 · 9 linksconcept

Data labeling

Human annotation of training data, a roughly $10 billion industry that reasoning traces from real usage now bypass, and the work Meta reassigned engineers to before its exodus.

needed by 1 · 8 linkstechnique

Distillation attacks

Training a model on a rival's outputs to copy its capability; the fear behind access cut-offs, Anthropic's countermeasures in Fable 5.1, and Zuckerberg's call to legalise it domestically.

9 linksconcept

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.

needed by 11 · 36 linksconcept

RL data industry

Vendors selling reinforcement-learning environments and training data to model labs, which Lambert says is taking off in China, partly through American vendors selling the same environments to Chinese labs.
