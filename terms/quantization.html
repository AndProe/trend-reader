Quantization

first seen 2026-W36 · 6 links · 1 min read

Reducing numeric precision of weights or activations after training (INT8, NF4, NVFP4) to cut memory and energy; treated in the sustainability paper as a systems choice rather than a monotonic trade-off.

Also seen as post-training quantization, low-precision serving, NVFP4, GGUF Q4

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Bryan Shan, Wenyao Gao, Daniel Nishball, Zane Fong (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-07) FP8 versus FP4 as the fairness line in hardware comparisons.
  • FuneralAI 葬AI (ChinaTalk, 2026-09-06) hobbyists deploying quantized 27B builds on Mac minis, 4090s and a modified 3080. Cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Technically, arXiv cs.AI).
  • Jainil Dharmil Shah (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-01) post-training compression as a systems choice rather than a monotonic trade-off.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-09-01) explained as audio compression; 4-bit as the default.
