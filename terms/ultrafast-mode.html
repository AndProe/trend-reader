Ultrafast mode

first seen 2026-W33 · 5 links · 1 min read

A paid high-speed inference tier (OpenAI's Cerebras-backed Ultrafast at 14 to 15x, Xiaomi's and Z.ai's high-speed tiers on TileRT) sold as a product; the market signal that users pay for latency.

Also seen as fast mode, ChatGPT Ultrafast, high-speed tier, UltraSpeed, HighSpeed

How the sources use it

  • Matthew Berman; Tibo Sottiaux (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-24) OpenAI's Cerebras-powered high-speed inference tier (also covered in Berman's 2026-08-14 roundup), detailed here from OpenAI's internal perspective: reserved mostly for external customers, used internally for incident response, and most effective on generation-heavy rather than tool-call-heavy workloads.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-19) Berman's and Tibo's term; CS-4 is the hardware behind it.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-14) OpenAI's Cerebras-accelerated inference mode for GPT-5.6 Sol, running roughly 14-15x faster at added cost.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-10) Berman's term; TileRT is one way to deliver it on GPUs.
