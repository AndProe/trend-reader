No-AI writing policy

first seen 2026-W36 · 6 links · 1 min read

A publication's rule that all text is written by humans, with AI allowed only for research and typo checks; The Pragmatic Engineer's stated stance and a marker of where readers expect provenance.

Also seen as human-written policy, no AI-generated text
