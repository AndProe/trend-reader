Codex

OpenAI's coding agent, used by Asana for migrations, cited as the enterprise token-share example, and the CLI agent in ToolGate; being merged into ChatGPT per Sottiaux.

needed by 2 · 15 linkstechnique

Sub-agents

Parallel agents spawned inside a run under a coordinator, now too many to read and the source of bursty serving traffic; dozens with bounded concurrency in Latent Space's Astra practice.

needed by 1 · 13 linksconcept

Agentic inference workloads

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns.

needed by 1 · 16 linksconcept

Vibe coding

Building software by prompting a coding agent and accepting what it produces without understanding the stack it chose; Technically's six-part series treats it as a practice whose failure modes (unexplained vendor choices, databases that fall over at scale, missing authorization) appear only when real users arrive, and argues the remedy is enough software-engineering vocabulary to ask the agent the right questions.

13 linksconcept

Vertical integration of AI labs

Frontier labs extending from models into the layers around them, buying developer toolchains (Anthropic and Bun, OpenAI and Astral), owning coding harnesses and cutting off third-party platforms, so that the product, not the model, is where they compete; the counter-position to model-agnostic architecture.

8 linksconcept

Specialised harnesses

Agent harnesses that bake in one domain's tools, prompts and guardrails and are sold as products, such as Harvey for legal research, Abridge for clinical notes, Sierra for customer service and Glean for enterprise search.
