Verification loops

first seen 2026-W35 · needed by 3 · 14 links · 1 min read

Pipelines that retry and validate each change until it passes tests, the common ingredient in every AI-migration success story and the basis of software factories.

Also seen as retry loops, validate-and-retry pipelines

How the sources use it

  • David Krevitt (Technically, 2026-09-22) continuous integration as automated checks on every commit, the practice he says coding agents are good at setting up. Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI, Gergely Orosz).
  • Yanzhang Ma, Zhenghan Tai, Hanwei Wu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Gergely Orosz); protected-case regression checks and negative controls gate each patch.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-17) agents asked for proof that a change would fail without it, in place of strict TDD. Recurring (W35).
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-08) guardrails (linting, coverage floors, post-deploy observability) replacing human review for low-risk changes.
  • Leitian Tao, Baolin Peng, Haorui Wang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-08) cross-source (Orosz); separating test construction from repair is a verification-loop design.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-27) (alias: retry loops) — the pipelines that retry and validate each file until it passes; the common ingredient in every success story.

Related terms

concept

AI-assisted migrations

Using coding agents to execute large framework, language or test-suite migrations that were previously impractical, with retry-and-verify pipelines doing most of the work.

11 linksconcept

Software factory

Agents running the reproduce, fix, review and verify loop end to end with humans setting rules and reviewing a fraction; the end state of loop engineering, in production at Vercel for 25 to 35 percent of merged PRs and contested as a metaphor by Orosz.

needed by 1 · 17 linkstechnique

Self-verification

An agent or model checking its own work: closing the loop with tests, telemetry, flags and screenshots at Ramp, or selecting among candidate solutions with a hidden-state reward model at inference.

6 linkstechnique

Internal benchmarks

Evals a company writes for its own workloads, the tests for non-deterministic behaviour, expected to merge with telemetry; Berman's standing advice against trusting vendor tables.

needed by 1 · 12 linkstechnique

Code review

The human review of changes before merge, now an overloaded ritual under AI-generated volume: product decisions stay human, validation moves to tooling, and some teams treat it as optional.

needed by 1 · 12 linkstechnique

TDD

Writing the test before the code and letting the failing test drive the implementation, an established practice now packaged as an agent skill.
