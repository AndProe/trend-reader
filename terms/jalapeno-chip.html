Jalapeno chip

first seen 2026-W35 · 7 links · 1 min read

OpenAI's Broadcom-built inference chip, designed from mid-2024 and taped out in 16 months, beating Blackwell on tokens per megawatt with single-token prediction; Rubin is the fair comparator and the results are OpenAI's own.

Also seen as Jalapeño, OpenAI inference chip, OpenAI Broadcom chip

Related terms

concept

Custom silicon

Accelerators designed by the companies that use them rather than bought from Nvidia: Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA, OpenAI's Jalapeño, Cerebras wafers for fast tiers.

needed by 2 · 9 linksmetric

Performance per watt

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

11 linkstechnique

Prefill-decode disaggregation

Serving prompt processing and token generation on separate hardware pools with KV cache transferred between them; behind Nvidia's best agentic results and absent by design from OpenAI's Jalapeño.

6 linksproduct

InferenceX

SemiAnalysis's inference benchmark suite (with AgentX for agentic workloads) run across Nvidia, AMD, Google and custom chips; the lab that verified Jalapeño's numbers.

5 linksproduct

Vera Rubin

Nvidia's next-generation GPU platform (Rubin, Rubin Ultra), shipping with HBM4, 5.4x the performance per megawatt of GB200, reportedly redesigned with less memory; the right comparator for Jalapeño.
