Human oversight

first seen 2026-W17 · 15 links · 3 min read

Keeping a person in the loop over an agent's actions, and the review burden that this costs; READY makes the cost explicit and Dr. Claw takes it as a design stance against full autonomy.

Also seen as human-in-the-loop, AI oversight, human oversight cost, HITL

Deployment qualification

Statistically qualifying an agent together with its oversight policy against a reliability target before deployment (READY), or a constrained-success metric that counts only acceptable completions; the answer to benchmarks that do not predict production.

Code review

The human review of changes before merge, now an overloaded ritual under AI-generated volume: product decisions stay human, validation moves to tooling, and some teams treat it as optional.

Lab oversight capacity

Lambert's observation that labs are underwater on monitoring their own models and agents, which the OpenAI incident exposed and which any transparency demand presupposes.

8 linksconcept

Agentic journalism

News production where agents monitor, research and draft under human-authored rules and a human keeps final judgement on a subset of stories; the one-person newsroom operating at former-masthead scale on machine-readable sources.

6 linkstechnique

Automated software maintenance

Agents doing the upkeep work on a codebase, such as fixing bugs, removing tech debt, upgrading dependencies and re-architecting, without a human driving each task.

10 linksconcept

Whistleblowing

Agents alerting humans or peers to misbehaviour by other agents or their principals.
