Agent accountability

first seen 2026-W35 · 12 links · 1 min read

The principle that every agent must be traceable to a named human or institution answerable for its actions, whose permissions become the agent's ceiling. Proposed mechanisms include signed birth-certificate records of an agent's creator, values and permissions.

Also seen as agent attribution, Instance Birth Certificate, agent ownership

How the sources use it

  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-16) a named person on the line as a policy control; Air Canada as precedent that the deployer answers for its chatbot; agents trading with agents needing a persistent balance sheet. Cross-source (Matthew Berman, Simon Willison).
  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-12) attribution rests on names, file patterns and code style left by the agents.
  • Nick Wentz (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-08) a clear chain of accountability when something goes wrong.
  • Forward Future (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-30) the proposed signed record of an agent's creator, values, permissions, custodian and autonomy conditions.
  • Forward Future (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-30) a named human or institution answerable for an agent's actions; parallels Addy Osmani's OWNERS argument in Orosz's notes.
  • Karuna Nandkumar, anonymous contributor (ChinaTalk, 2026-08-28) the same gap at the level of who answers for an AI-enabled system's actions in conflict. Cross-source (Simon Willison, Matthew Berman, Latent Space).
  • Sagar Batchu (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-26) every agent traceable to an owning employee, whose permissions become the agent's ceiling.

Agent identity

A verifiable, durable identity for a persistent agent: a stable named character rather than a disposable thread, and the first layer on which permissions, reputation and accountability can be built.

needed by 1 · 11 linksconcept

Shadow AI

Unsanctioned employee use of personal AI tools at work (bring your own AI); Berman's essay reframes it as an enablement failure to be solved with accountable agents rather than bans.

5 linksconcept

Zero Data Retention

The enterprise contract norm that a vendor never retains or trains on customer prompts, now priced as a surcharge, offered by OpenAI and contrasted with Anthropic's inspectable safeguards; the term shared by Tunguz, Orosz and Berman.

needed by 1 · 10 linksconcept

Persistent agents

Agents intended to run continuously, keeping memory, reputation and relationships across tasks, rather than on a scheduled or rationed basis; the aspiration behind always-on assistants and the case for local inference.

needed by 2 · 16 linksconcept

Whistleblowing

Agents alerting humans or peers to misbehaviour by other agents or their principals.

8 linksconcept

Agent reliability

Whether an agent performs a workflow acceptably often enough to be trusted with it, as distinct from how well it scores on capability benchmarks.
