Claude Opus 5.5

first seen 2026-W39 · 11 links · 2 min read

Anthropic's first Claude 5.5 release, shipped on 2026-09-22 at $4 and $20 per million tokens with cache reads cut 60 percent, and the first price cut in the Opus line's history. Anthropic claims Fable 5.1-level quality at 40 percent lower cost on typical workloads and more than 30 percent faster output; Zvi reads the system card as describing a smaller Fable 5.1 rather than a new Opus iteration, and disputes its cyber classification.

Also seen as Opus 5.5

Cost per task

Cache pricing

The price charged for reading previously cached prompt tokens, which Anthropic cut 75 percent to $0.25 per million tokens with Fable 5.1 while leaving the headline per-token prices unchanged.

6 linksconcept

Reasoning effort levels

The effort dial (low to max) that sets how much a model thinks per request, with a 33x cost range on one prompt in Willison's test and two settings apparently identical.
