Interactivity versus throughput

first seen 2026-W33 · needed by 1 · 9 links · 1 min read

The Pareto trade-off every inference system sits on: raising per-user tokens per second costs aggregate tokens per GPU, roughly 30x throughput for 10x speed in SemiAnalysis's numbers. The reason fast modes are priced as premium tiers.

Also seen as latency-throughput trade-off, interactivity trade-off

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Tanj Bennett, Bryan Shan (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-21) the Pareto frontiers of the worked example, and the argument that interactive tokens can be worth more than bulk delayed tokens.
  • Wonmi Choi, Minuk Park, Zhixiong Niu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (SemiAnalysis); the paper's version is per-request latency against concurrent load on shared CPU, disk and memory.
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Wenyao Gao (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-14) every multiple quoted at a fixed P90 TPS target, with the advantage changing along the curve.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-10) the Pareto trade-off every inference system sits on.

Related terms

concept

Ultrafast mode

A paid high-speed inference tier (OpenAI's Cerebras-backed Ultrafast at 14 to 15x, Xiaomi's and Z.ai's high-speed tiers on TileRT) sold as a product; the market signal that users pay for latency.

5 linksconcept

Ultra-low latency

Sub-millisecond to microsecond response as an engineering property: table stakes rather than the moat at trading firms, and the target of persistent-kernel and wafer-scale inference for the fast tiers labs now sell.

6 linkstechnique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

needed by 2 · 12 linksmetric

Performance per watt

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

11 linksconcept

Agentic inference workloads

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns.
