Bullwhip effect

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 1 · 6 links · 1 min read

Supply-chain amplification in which demand swings grow larger upstream; Tunguz applies it to AI hardware as sequential multi-year shortages cascading from GPUs to memory to CPUs, storage and physical shells.

Also seen as AI hardware bullwhip, supply-chain amplification

How the sources use it

  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-10) the GPU, then memory, then CPU shortage sequence Orosz describes; the label is Tunguz's, not Orosz's. New for this source;.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-23) sequential, multi-year hardware shortages cascading from GPUs to memory to CPUs to storage to physical shells.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-23) the supply-chain theory (Tunguz's own 2020 framing) applied to physical AI hardware.
