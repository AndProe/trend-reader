GPT-5.6 Sol

first seen 2026-W33 · 3 links · 1 min read

OpenAI's mainline model before Astra, with Sol, Terra and Luna government variants and the Cerebras-backed Ultrafast tier; outperforms Fable 5 on revenue share despite lower per-token price.

Also seen as GPT-5.6, Sol

How the sources use it

  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-22) the price point Opus 5.5 matched before OpenAI halved it. Recurring and cross-source.
  • Nikita Agarwal, Nivedit Jain (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Latent Space, Matthew Berman, Tomasz Tunguz); all three GPT-5.6 tiers are measured, with Sol the strongest.
  • Haozhe Liu, Tian Ye, Sensen Gao et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) used in this note; see Evidence.
  • Afshin Khadangi (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-13) cross-source; the two models whose sketched successor architectures are said to overlap.
  • Ali Ansari, Haoran Sun, Andy Zeyi Liu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-11) cross-source; the model whose corrected scores are reported.
  • Leitian Tao, Baolin Peng, Haorui Wang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-08) cross-source; its tests set the upper reference for feedback quality.
  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-07) the OpenAI baseline for the Sol-to-Astra flips.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-31) OpenAI's model with Sol, Terra and Luna government variants.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-14) OpenAI's priciest mainline tier, outperforming Fable 5 on revenue share despite lower per-token price.
