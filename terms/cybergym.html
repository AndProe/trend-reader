CyberGym

first seen 2026-W36 · 8 links · 1 min read

A benchmark of real-world vulnerability discovery and exploitation tasks used to rank agentic cyber capability, with a public leaderboard. Google DeepMind cites frontier-level results on it for its gated Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber variant, and the Feyospace team reports a verified 63.24 percent success rate at 10th place for open-weight models trained by a seven-person team.

Also seen as CyberGym leaderboard

How the sources use it

  • The Feyospace authors (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-08) the leaderboard position (10th, 63.24 percent verified) used to argue that open-weight cyber capability is limited by environments and teacher access rather than scale.
  • Zongjie Li, Alan Z. W, John Nicolas J et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-08) recurring mention (Frontier Labs); the leaderboard the checkpoints rank on.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-02) the benchmark on which the lab reports frontier-level vulnerability discovery for its Cyber variant, gated through the Fairwind programme.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-02) used in this note; see Evidence.
