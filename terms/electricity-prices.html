Electricity prices

first seen 2026-W33 · 7 links · 1 min read

Retail power prices rising with data-centre demand (6.9 percent in one tally, 17 to 24 percent bill increases in PJM) and the risk of gas prices tripling; SemiAnalysis attributes most of the PJM rise to auction design rather than load.

Also seen as power prices, ratepayer bills, gas price shock

How the sources use it

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-17) local power prices as the stated public worry behind the alliance.
  • Dylan Patel, Maya Barkin, Reyk Knuhtsen, Jeremie Eliahou Ontiveros (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-15) PJM-driven bill increases in Ohio as the trigger for its moratorium wave. Recurring and cross-source (Berman).
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-31) 6.9 percent rise tied to data-center demand.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-17) data-center electricity demand potentially tripling natural gas prices.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-16) Berman's term; here the mechanism behind the 17 to 24 percent bill increases.

Hyperscalers

Performance per watt

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

Grid interconnection

Connecting a data centre or power plant to the utility grid through the queue a grid operator runs, which SemiAnalysis's energy team treats as the constraint behind-the-meter power routes around.

Data center jobs

Employment created by the AI buildout in data-center construction, power infrastructure and skilled trades, set against jobs lost to AI.
