Ambient AI

first seen 2026-W35 · 7 links · 1 min read

AI that is continuously present in devices and products rather than consulted on demand. Maxime Labonne ties it to on-device models embedded in appliances, cars and industrial equipment, where checking again costs nothing per token, while Nick Wentz uses it for agents built so far into products that users never think about them.

Also seen as ambient assistants, ambient monitoring

How the sources use it

  • Alex Salkever (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-22) the author's case for always-present assistance in glasses or earbuds.
  • Nick Wentz (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-08) agents embedded in products so users never think about them. Recurring label.
  • Maxime Labonne (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-26) AI embedded in devices/appliances/industrial equipment that watches continuously rather than being invoked on demand. New/rising.

Persistent agents

Agents intended to run continuously, keeping memory, reputation and relationships across tasks, rather than on a scheduled or rationed basis; the aspiration behind always-on assistants and the case for local inference.

On-device inference

Running models locally on the user's machine or on-premise, now competitive with cloud frontier models for most tasks at the cost of thinking time, and eliminating per-token metering for always-on agents.

needed by 1 · 13 linksconcept

Edge AI

Running inference on local hardware (Macs, single GPUs, devices) rather than in a data centre, driven by cheap capable open models; Tunguz's mainframe-to-personal analogy.

Small language models

Personal agent workflows
