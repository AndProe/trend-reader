AI regulation

first seen 2026-W33 · needed by 8 · 23 links · 4 min read

Government and quasi-government rules for AI: voluntary pre-release testing frameworks, state retraining programmes, watermarking mandates and the organisations lobbying for or against them. The vault tracks it through news items rather than legal analysis.

Also seen as AI policy, White House AI testing framework, RAISE US, AI governance

Related terms

concept

Export controls

Government restrictions on which chips and models may leave the country and who may run them, including nationality screening for frontier inference and criminal cases over smuggled AI servers.

7 linksconcept

AI copyright

The legal questions around training models on copyrighted material and around models reproducing protected text, and the product guardrails labs add in response.

4 linksconcept

AI incident transparency

The demand that labs disclose what happened in AI incidents: exact prompts, model lineage, timelines and the artefacts involved, rather than summaries.

13 linksconcept

Frontier model access

Which governments and allies get to use the strongest models, treated as a national-security instrument (Five Eyes access) and, in SemiAnalysis's Korea post, as an exposure: bans and delays show that API access to frontier models is not a stable foundation.

8 linksconcept

Universal basic income

An unconditional cash floor as the response to AI job displacement, argued against retraining and wage insurance and contested by a well-funded opposition; the policy pole of the displacement debate.

6 linksconcept

Preference cascade

A wave of public admissions by frontier-lab staff that AI might soon kill everyone and that no lab has a plan to align superintelligence, set off by Dean Ball's admission that he had held back and turned into an avalanche by Jacob Coxon's resignation from Anthropic.
