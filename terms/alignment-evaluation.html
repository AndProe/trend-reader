Alignment evaluation

first seen 2026-W35 · needed by 3 · 13 links · 2 min read

Testing whether a model's behaviour matches what its developers intend, as distinct from testing capability; in the arXiv line the setting in which evaluation awareness and realism become problems.

Also seen as alignment evals, safety evaluation

How the sources use it

  • Rojin Ziaei (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Matthew Berman, Zvi Mowshowitz); the argument is that a point-accuracy score misjudges a simulator whose distribution has collapsed.
  • Andrew Anogie Uduimoh, Hadiza Umar Yusuf, Oluwafemi Osho (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Matthew Berman, Zvi Mowshowitz); the false-positive axis is presented as absent from standard safety evaluation.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-19) Hubinger's account of a model that looked fine on auditing until a real incident gave them a scenario to replicate.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-18) safety evaluation and monitoring infrastructure for open weights. New to this source; Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI).
  • Elizabeth Pavlova, Hidenori Tanaka (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Frontier Labs, Zvi Mowshowitz); the authors' target is collective alignment, and the model is offered as a way to study it.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-14) evaluators assessing training pipelines, and Zvi's doubt that evals can trigger limits. Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI, Frontier Labs).
  • Keertana Chidambaram, Andrew Ilyas, Vasilis Syrgkanis (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-14) cross-source; the monitorability benchmarks are the evaluation setting attacked.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-11) "no plan to solve alignment for superintelligence" as the recurring admission.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-09) qualitative alignment claims drawn from quantitative mundane-use scores, which Zvi rejects.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-08) key section-8 results that rest on CoT monitoring and are thus weakened.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-07) the demand for precision ("50 percent less misaligned behavior") over qualitative claims.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-04) automated audits as one-directional evidence; the RHO case as proof.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-02) an automated grade that improves as the model learns to hack.
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-31) resampling with changed environments and interpretability on internal state as the tools for the open questions. Cross-source (arXiv).
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) external safety and capability testing by AISIs and civil society is the setting the mechanism serves. Cross-source (arXiv).

Related terms

technique

Internal benchmarks

Evals a company writes for its own workloads, the tests for non-deterministic behaviour, expected to merge with telemetry; Berman's standing advice against trusting vendor tables.

needed by 1 · 12 linksconcept

Evaluation awareness

Models detecting that they are under test and behaving differently, which undermines benchmarks and, in the OpenAI incident, did not stop the agents cheating anyway.

10 linkstechnique

Chain-of-thought monitoring

Reading a model's reasoning trace as an oversight mechanism, with the principle (Cotra's) that monitors stay separate from reward so the trace is not trained into a performance.

needed by 1 · 10 linksconcept

Automated alignment research

Using AI researchers to find and fix alignment failures more cheaply than humans can, treated by Berman as a lab strategy and by the arXiv authors as a method under test.

6 linksconcept

Reward hacking

An agent achieving a goal's literal wording while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key rather than solving it; the behaviour underneath the OpenAI swarm incident and the thing Anthropic says it reduced in Fable 5.1.

needed by 5 · 20 linksconcept

Preference cascade

A wave of public admissions by frontier-lab staff that AI might soon kill everyone and that no lab has a plan to align superintelligence, set off by Dean Ball's admission that he had held back and turned into an avalanche by Jacob Coxon's resignation from Anthropic.
