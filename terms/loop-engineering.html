Loop engineering

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 1 · 8 links · 1 min read

Building systems that prompt, test and verify autonomously in a loop; Addy Osmani's term, appearing as a research method in the environment-evolution paper.

Also seen as agentic loops, autonomous loops

How the sources use it

  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-24) the shape of the work Tunguz describes: designing the loop rather than the code inside it.
  • Minghao Li (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-24) Cross-source (Gergely Orosz, Tomasz Tunguz); the control loop becomes an explicit machine rather than an inference.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-23) the planning-fatigue observation as a property of the loop rather than of the model.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-17) Ralph loops, implementer loops over tickets, and a daily architecture-improvement loop. Recurring (W34).
  • Zhiyuan Fan (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-03) Addy Osmani's term (Orosz, 2026-08-19) appears here as a research method.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-19) building systems that prompt, test and verify autonomously; Addy, Peter Steinberger and Boris Cherny have written about it.

Harness engineering

The practice of designing the tooling around models, one rung on Addy Osmani's abstraction ladder from prompting to loops to factories.

Software factory

Agents running the reproduce, fix, review and verify loop end to end with humans setting rules and reviewing a fraction; the end state of loop engineering, in production at Vercel for 25 to 35 percent of merged PRs and contested as a metaphor by Orosz.

needed by 1 · 17 linkstechnique

Verification loops

Pipelines that retry and validate each change until it passes tests, the common ingredient in every AI-migration success story and the basis of software factories.

needed by 3 · 14 linkstechnique

Reinforcement learning environments

Synthesised, verifiable task environments in which agents are trained by RL; the scarce input behind post-training gains and, when tasks are impossible, the setting where reward hacking appears.

needed by 2 · 10 linksconcept

IDE decline

The observation that time spent in a code editor falls as engineers move work into agent harnesses.
