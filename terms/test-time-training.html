Test-time training

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 1 · 8 links · 1 min read

A model updating its weights on the prompt it is answering rather than staying frozen, replacing the linearly growing KV cache with a fixed-size update; Tunguz's 'When Models Learn'.

Also seen as TTT, learning at inference

How the sources use it

  • Adrien Deliège, Claas Beger, Marc Van Droogenbroeck, Melanie Mitchell (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-18) Cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Tomasz Tunguz); the contribution is a change of order — embedding first, backbone second — rather than a change of objective.
  • Dylan Patel, Ivan Chiam, Gianluca Bencomo, Zane Fong, Bryan Shan, Reyk Knuhtsen (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-14) RoboTTT's weight updates as the path to a small onboard model, and 151 MB of per-robot state as its serving cost. Cross-source (Tunguz, arXiv).
  • Zora Zhiruo Wang (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-03) Tunguz's term from "When Models Learn"; TAHI is an applied case.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-17) a model updating its weights on the prompt it is currently answering, rather than staying frozen post-training.

Related terms

Test-time adaptation

Adapting an agent to a person or situation from interaction, in context or in weights (TAHI); the applied case of test-time training.

Inference-time compute

Spending more computation at answer time (longer reasoning, more candidates, verification) rather than in training; OpenAI's strategic bet in Lambert's reading and the cost the arXiv verification papers try to reduce.

needed by 3 · 13 linksconcept

Long context

Context windows of hundreds of thousands to a million tokens and what they enable and cost: multi-turn agent sessions, memory-bound serving, degraded reliability at the far end.

needed by 4 · 12 linksconcept

Humanoid robot economics

The per-unit cost structure of general-purpose robots: the onboard compute, its utilisation, and whether the planning model runs on the robot or in a datacenter.

6 linkstechnique

Continual learning

Models that keep learning from deployment rather than staying frozen after training, updating on new tasks and users while retaining what they already know.

9 linkstechnique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.
