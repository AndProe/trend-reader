Universal basic income

first seen 2026-W33 · 6 links · 1 min read

An unconditional cash floor as the response to AI job displacement, argued against retraining and wage insurance and contested by a well-funded opposition; the policy pole of the displacement debate.

Also seen as UBI, basic income

How the sources use it

  • Jack Clark (Import AI, 2026-09-07) among the three least popular policies. Cross-source (Berman); new to this source.
  • Jordan Schneider (ChinaTalk, 2026-08-24) named as one endpoint of the redistribution debate, against a jobs guarantee he thinks more likely. Cross-source (Import AI, Matthew Berman).
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-17) the policy fight over UBI as a response to AI job displacement, with a well-funded opposing organization.
  • Scott Santens (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-16) the unconditional cash-floor policy the essay contrasts against retraining, citing extensive pilot evidence.
