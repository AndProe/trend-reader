Revenue-share licenses

first seen 2026-W31 · 7 links · 1 min read

Open-model licences that are free to experiment with and require a commercial agreement, security review or revenue share above a threshold (Kimi K3 at $20 million a year); 'open until you scale'. The mechanism by which open models might pay for themselves.

Also seen as revenue-triggered licensing, open until you scale, commercial-threshold licenses

How the sources use it

  • Florian Brand (Interconnects, 2026-09-08) GLM-5.3's $10 billion security-review clause joins Kimi K3 and MiniMax M3. Recurring (first seen W31 in this source).
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-01) Interconnects' term; the Kimi K3 $20 million threshold.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-31) (alias: open until you scale) — free for experimentation, commercial license and security review above a revenue threshold.
  • David Krevitt (Technically, 2026-08-09) the $20 million threshold explained as freemium between lab and provider.
  • Florian Brand (Interconnects, 2026-08-02) Kimi K3's commercial-agreement requirement and its policy exposure.

Commoditise your complement

Moonshot's open-weights model whose licence requires a commercial agreement above $20 million of model-as-a-service revenue; the reference case for revenue-share licences and, in SemiAnalysis's study, for open models doing frontier agentic work.
