Preference cascade

first seen 2026-W37 · 8 links · 2 min read

A wave of public admissions by frontier-lab staff that AI might soon kill everyone and that no lab has a plan to align superintelligence, set off by Dean Ball's admission that he had held back and turned into an avalanche by Jacob Coxon's resignation from Anthropic. Zvi uses it for the moment private worry across OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind became public, with attributed extinction estimates from about 10 to 50 percent and calls for coordinated slowdown.

Also seen as extinction risk preference cascade, cascade of admissions
