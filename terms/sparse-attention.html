Sparse attention

first seen 2026-W36 · 6 links · 1 min read

Attention mechanisms that attend to a subset of positions so that long contexts stay tractable; A.X K2's long-context mechanism.

Also seen as sparse attention mechanisms

Related terms

concept

Long context

Context windows of hundreds of thousands to a million tokens and what they enable and cost: multi-turn agent sessions, memory-bound serving, degraded reliability at the far end.

needed by 4 · 12 linkstechnique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

needed by 2 · 12 linkstechnique

Mixture of experts

An architecture that activates only a fraction of parameters per token (GLM 5.3 Flash: 320B total, 18B active), the mechanism behind cheap capable open models.

7 linksconcept

Sovereign AI models

Frontier-scale models trained by or for a state in its own language and under its own control (A.X K2, Korea's tournament models), motivated by the exposure of depending on American frontier APIs.

10 linksmodel

Qwen

Alibaba's open-weight model family, the most downloaded on Hugging Face and the most common base for derivative models.
