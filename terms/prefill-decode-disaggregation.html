Prefill-decode disaggregation

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 1 min read

Serving prompt processing and token generation on separate hardware pools with KV cache transferred between them; behind Nvidia's best agentic results and absent by design from OpenAI's Jalapeño.

Also seen as PD disaggregation, disaggregated serving

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Tanj Bennett, Bryan Shan (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-21) the default architecture here, with aggregation argued as the special case in section 19.
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Cam Quilici, Alec Ibarra, Kimbo Chen, Myron Xie (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-18) keeping Engram for prefill gave more correct answers than keeping it for decode, which the authors attribute to a richer KV cache handed to decode workers.
  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Harrison Barclay, Minjae Kang (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-13) the analysis covers only the decode pool; the experiment runs a disaggregated 8-plus-8 setup.
  • Arya Tschand, Yaosheng Fu, Vikram Sharma Mailthody et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-11) cross-source (SemiAnalysis); the paper generalises disaggregation to split decode by attention type.
  • Dylan Patel, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Bryan Shan, Wenyao Gao, Daniel Nishball, Zane Fong (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-07) the missing piece in the external TPU stack.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-24) the serving technique behind Nvidia's best points.

Related terms

technique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

needed by 2 · 12 linksconcept

Agentic inference workloads

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns.

needed by 1 · 16 linksproduct

Jalapeno chip

OpenAI's Broadcom-built inference chip, designed from mid-2024 and taped out in 16 months, beating Blackwell on tokens per megawatt with single-token prediction; Rubin is the fair comparator and the results are OpenAI's own.

7 linksconcept

Interactivity versus throughput

The Pareto trade-off every inference system sits on: raising per-user tokens per second costs aggregate tokens per GPU, roughly 30x throughput for 10x speed in SemiAnalysis's numbers.

needed by 1 · 9 linksconcept

CUDA moat

Nvidia's software advantage: the CUDA stack and the day-0 framework support that make its GPUs the default target for every serving engine, which competitors must match before their hardware counts.
