Performance per watt

first seen 2026-W34 · 11 links · 1 min read

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

Also seen as intelligence per watt, tokens per megawatt, perf per watt

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Wenyao Gao (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-14) tokens per second per MW as the metric where the authors dispute Nvidia's own claim. Recurring and cross-source (Tunguz).
  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Harrison Barclay, Minjae Kang (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-13) tokens per watt as the existing scarcity metric the authors extend to tokens per HBM wafer. Cross-source (Tunguz).
  • Arya Tschand, Yaosheng Fu, Vikram Sharma Mailthody et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-11) cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Tunguz); the efficiency framing of the tokens-per-joule result.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-25) the metric OpenAI designs for.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-21) the efficiency metric (analogous to Koomey's computations per joule) applied to AI models.
  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Wega Chu, Jordan Nanos (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-19) 125 to 135 kW per rack for double the performance.

Jalapeno chip

OpenAI's Broadcom-built inference chip, designed from mid-2024 and taped out in 16 months, beating Blackwell on tokens per megawatt with single-token prediction; Rubin is the fair comparator and the results are OpenAI's own.

Custom silicon

Accelerators designed by the companies that use them rather than bought from Nvidia: Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA, OpenAI's Jalapeño, Cerebras wafers for fast tiers.

Behind-the-meter power

Generation built on the data-centre site, bypassing the grid and its capacity markets; where developers go when interconnection queues and auction prices make grid power slow or expensive.

9 linksmetric

Energy efficiency

Measured GPU energy per unit of model work, treated as a first-class pillar in the triple-bottom-line sustainability paper; the research-side twin of performance per watt.

4 linksmetric

Revenue per gigawatt

Revenue earned per unit of power capacity: Tunguz's $50 million per megawatt against $10 to 15 million of cost, SemiAnalysis's $100 billion-plus per gigawatt-year for frontier APIs, Nvidia's metric for owning more of each AI factory.

8 linksconcept

Humanoid robot economics

The per-unit cost structure of general-purpose robots: the onboard compute, its utilisation, and whether the planning model runs on the robot or in a datacenter.
