Codex

first seen 2026-W20 · needed by 2 · 15 links · 2 min read

Related terms

product

Claude Code

Anthropic's terminal coding agent, the November 2025 inflection SemiAnalysis dates the agentic era from, the source of the AgentX traces, and the harness compared against by Ramp and Orosz's guests.

9 linksconcept

AI-assisted migrations

Using coding agents to execute large framework, language or test-suite migrations that were previously impractical, with retry-and-verify pipelines doing most of the work.

11 linksconcept

Personal agent workflows

Individuals using a single agent or chief-of-staff bot to triage email, summarise meetings, coordinate the day and delegate to sub-bots over chat; the consumer-side pattern Grok Bot and Orosz's inbox agent illustrate, and Sottiaux's rationale for a universal personal agent.

15 linksconcept

Vertical integration of AI labs

Frontier labs extending from models into the layers around them, buying developer toolchains (Anthropic and Bun, OpenAI and Astral), owning coding harnesses and cutting off third-party platforms, so that the product, not the model, is where they compete; the counter-position to model-agnostic architecture.

8 linkstechnique

Automated software maintenance

Agents doing the upkeep work on a codebase, such as fixing bugs, removing tech debt, upgrading dependencies and re-architecting, without a human driving each task.

10 linksmetric

Enterprise agent tokens

The share of enterprise customers' tokens going to agentic tools rather than chat, reported by OpenAI at 64 percent for Codex against ChatGPT by June 2026.
