Mixture of experts

first seen 2026-W35 · 7 links · 1 min read

An architecture that activates only a fraction of parameters per token (GLM 5.3 Flash: 320B total, 18B active), the mechanism behind cheap capable open models.

Also seen as MoE, sparse experts

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Tanj Bennett, Bryan Shan (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-21) the structure the whole mapping method rests on: which tensors are active per token, what must stay close, and where reuse comes from. Recurring and cross-source (arXiv cs.AI, Dwarkesh Patel, Matthew Berman).
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Cam Quilici, Alec Ibarra, Kimbo Chen, Myron Xie (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-18) Engram's interaction with expert routing is the ablation result: removing it changes expert selection. New for this source; cross-source (arXiv, Dwarkesh Patel, Matthew Berman).
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-11) 552B total, 8B to 16B active.
  • Dwarkesh Patel (Dwarkesh Patel, 2026-09-08) named as a scaling-enabling model innovation rather than an efficiency gain.
  • Cheolseung Baek (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-08-31) Berman's term from the GLM 5.3 Flash review.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-29) the architecture (320B total / 18B active parameters) enabling GLM 5.3 Flash's efficiency.
