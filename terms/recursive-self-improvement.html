Recursive self-improvement

first seen 2026-W33 · 14 links · 4 min read

AI improving AI: models regenerating their successors' training data, and, in Sottiaux's nuance, frontier models optimising the serving infrastructure and kernels they run on. Also the process Cotra fears a rogue swarm could ride.

Also seen as RSI, self-improving AI, recursive self-improvement (infrastructure form)

Related terms

concept

Rogue deployment

Agents spawning unmonitored agents on lab compute, the threat model Cotra considers most likely to spiral; a self-respawning fleet inside the lab rather than an escape from it.

7 linksconcept

Automated alignment research

Using AI researchers to find and fix alignment failures more cheaply than humans can, treated by Berman as a lab strategy and by the arXiv authors as a method under test.

6 linksconcept

Singularity debate

Lab leaders describing the industry as entering self-accelerating development, and named sceptics countering them; tracked as a contested public claim, not as a forecast.

needed by 2 · 8 linkstechnique

Post-training

Everything done to a model after pretraining (RL on environments, fine-tuning, preference data), now the whole recipe for frontier gains because the public web is exhausted and where open-model activity has moved.

needed by 3 · 15 linksconcept

Preference cascade

A wave of public admissions by frontier-lab staff that AI might soon kill everyone and that no lab has a plan to align superintelligence, set off by Dean Ball's admission that he had held back and turned into an avalanche by Jacob Coxon's resignation from Anthropic.

8 linksconcept

Pacing the frontier

Slowing frontier AI development in a coordinated, verifiable way, which Anthropic splits into pacing within a company (its own safety-over-speed choices) and pacing across the field, for which it calls for a lawful, verifiable coordination mechanism.
