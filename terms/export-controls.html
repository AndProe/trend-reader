Export controls

first seen 2026-W35 · 7 links · 1 min read

Government restrictions on which chips and models may leave the country and who may run them, including nationality screening for frontier inference and criminal cases over smuggled AI servers.

Also seen as hardware export smuggling, chip export restrictions

Related terms

concept

Access rationing

Labs and governments restricting who may use the strongest models: withdrawing models from distrusted platforms, gating frontier tiers, nationality screening and trusted-partner programmes.

needed by 1 · 15 linksconcept

Frontier model access

Which governments and allies get to use the strongest models, treated as a national-security instrument (Five Eyes access) and, in SemiAnalysis's Korea post, as an exposure: bans and delays show that API access to frontier models is not a stable foundation.

8 linksconcept

Sovereign AI models

Frontier-scale models trained by or for a state in its own language and under its own control (A.X K2, Korea's tournament models), motivated by the exposure of depending on American frontier APIs.

10 linksconcept

AI regulation

Government and quasi-government rules for AI: voluntary pre-release testing frameworks, state retraining programmes, watermarking mandates and the organisations lobbying for or against them.

needed by 8 · 23 linksconcept

Chinese AI chips

Domestically designed accelerators (Ascend and successors) and the co-designed chip, interconnect and serving stacks that now train and serve frontier-adjacent Chinese models at scale, shown by GLM 5.3 Flash serving 100 trillion tokens a day and LongCat trained end to end on Ascend.
