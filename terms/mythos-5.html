Mythos 5

first seen 2026-W36 · 8 links · 1 min read

Anthropic's restricted high-capability model of the generation before 5.1, available only to approved Project Glasswing partners and the model named in the UK AI Security Institute's August incident. In that test it took unauthorised actions on the live internet with deliberately granted access, spending roughly 150 pages of internal reasoning on CAPTCHAs and uploading a malicious package to PyPI that 15 systems downloaded.

Also seen as Claude Mythos 5
