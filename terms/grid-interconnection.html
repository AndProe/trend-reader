Grid interconnection

first seen 2026-W33 · 7 links · 1 min read

Connecting a data centre or power plant to the utility grid through the queue a grid operator runs, which SemiAnalysis's energy team treats as the constraint behind-the-meter power routes around. In the PJM post the authors note that interconnection fast-tracks have not energised a megawatt, and in the behind-the-meter series they describe cheap grid interconnections as disappearing while new grid generation takes five years or more.

Also seen as interconnection queue, interconnection fast-tracks, grid interconnections
