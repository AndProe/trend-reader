On-device inference

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 1 · 13 links · 2 min read

Running models locally on the user's machine or on-premise, now competitive with cloud frontier models for most tasks at the cost of thinking time, and eliminating per-token metering for always-on agents.

Also seen as local models, local inference, on-premise models

Related terms

concept

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.

needed by 11 · 36 linksconcept

Edge AI

Running inference on local hardware (Macs, single GPUs, devices) rather than in a data centre, driven by cheap capable open models; Tunguz's mainframe-to-personal analogy.

9 linksconcept

Small language models

Models designed small from the start rather than compressed from large ones, evaluated in the arXiv sustainability line for energy and quality per parameter.

8 linkstechnique

Quantization

Reducing numeric precision of weights or activations after training (INT8, NF4, NVFP4) to cut memory and energy; treated in the sustainability paper as a systems choice rather than a monotonic trade-off.

7 linksconcept

Persistent agents

Agents intended to run continuously, keeping memory, reputation and relationships across tasks, rather than on a scheduled or rationed basis; the aspiration behind always-on assistants and the case for local inference.

needed by 2 · 16 linkstechnique

Smart model routing

Sending each request to the cheapest model that can handle it, frontier reserved for hard cases; Ramp's router, OpenRouter's marketplace and the mechanism behind local-model cost ceilings.
