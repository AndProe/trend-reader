AI revenue requirement

first seen 2026-W36 · 6 links · 3 min read

The AI revenue requirement is a back-of-the-envelope figure with large consequences. Start from the borrowing needed to build AI data centres, roughly $4 trillion over five years, apply the interest and depreciation such assets carry, and the industry must earn on the order of $1.2 to $1.5 trillion a year to service them. A separate published estimate puts the figure at $2.5 trillion. Current AI revenue across all vendors is a small fraction of either number.

For an executive this is the sanity check on the whole cycle. It says how much of the world's software, advertising and services spending would have to move to AI for the investment to pay, and over what period. If the number is unreachable, the losses fall on lenders and investors and, later, on customers whose vendors retrench. If it is reachable, AI is on course to become one of the largest industries in history. Pricing, valuations and the timing of public offerings all implicitly assume an answer.

Also seen as revenue needed to cover AI capex

Deeper dive

What it measures. The requirement is the revenue at which debt service and capital recovery on AI infrastructure break even. The derivation runs from the financing side: take the projected capital spending, assume the leverage typical of project finance, apply the cost of that debt and a depreciation schedule for chips that lose value in a few years, and the annual cash flow needed to carry the stack follows; see AI infrastructure debt for the borrowing that drives it. The $1.2 to $1.5 trillion range comes from that method. The $2.5 trillion figure starts from total capital expenditure and asks what return investors would need. Both numbers are annual and both are far above present AI revenue, which is why the gap rather than either figure is the finding.

Why it matters now. The build-out is being financed ahead of the revenue, on the credit of a few Hyperscalers and labs. The requirement makes that bet legible: it can be compared with the size of software, cloud and advertising markets to see how much substitution would be needed. It also connects to unit economics through Revenue per gigawatt, the revenue a facility must generate for each unit of power it draws, which is the same requirement expressed per site. Investors use the number to interrogate AI valuations, and it frames what a prospective Anthropic IPO or other lab listing must promise about growth.

Trade-offs. The figure is sensitive to assumptions that are all uncertain: how fast chips depreciate, what leverage lenders accept, whether facilities are repurposed if AI demand falls short, and whether revenue counts only model sales or also the value captured inside products such as search and advertising. A requirement expressed as one number invites false precision. It is best used as a range and a direction.

What to watch. Reported AI revenue growth rates against the requirement, chip depreciation assumptions in lab and hyperscaler accounts, and whether any large offtaker renegotiates capacity commitments.

concept

AI infrastructure debt

The borrowing required to build AI data centres, estimated at roughly $4 trillion over five years, and the instruments that carry it: project finance at 65 to 75 percent leverage, off-balance-sheet vehicles, private credit, commercial paper, corporate and municipal bonds.

Revenue per gigawatt

Revenue per gigawatt is the annual revenue an AI operator earns per unit of electrical capacity it has built out, with power rather than chips or servers as the denominator.

Hyperscalers

Hyperscalers are the largest cloud providers, AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, together with Oracle and increasingly the frontier labs themselves, that buy most AI accelerators, issue most AI-related debt and build the gigawatt campuses.

AI valuations

Revenue multiples for AI companies, public and private, and the gap between them and the rest of software: 100x-type multiples returning at the $100 million revenue milestone while public software has compressed from a 100x ceiling to about 34x.

Anthropic IPO
