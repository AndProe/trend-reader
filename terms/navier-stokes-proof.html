Navier-Stokes proof

first seen 2026-W37 · 9 links · 1 min read

OpenAI's announced resolution of the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the Millennium Prize Problems, produced by agents running an unreleased model more capable than GPT-6 Astra and verified in Lean with Astra, as reported by Willison and Berman. Berman reads it as evidence of new knowledge on the path to recursive self-improvement, while Willison and Stratechery's Sharp foreground the credit dispute with Buckmaster and Alpöge and the unanswered question of whether their Codex drafts fed OpenAI's models.

Also seen as Navier-Stokes result, Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem
