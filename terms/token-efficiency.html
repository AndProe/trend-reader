Token efficiency

first seen 2026-W34 · 9 links · 2 min read

Capability per token consumed or produced: the gap between intelligence rank and verbosity rank, the 30-to-1 input-to-output ratio of agentic work, capability per training token in A.X K2.

Also seen as input-to-output token ratio, verbosity, tokens per task

How the sources use it

  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-22) Shihipar's claim for Opus 5.5, against a max-level run that exhausted a 128,000-token output limit.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-21) output tokens per task read as intelligence density, traded off against price per token.
  • Haozhe Liu, Tian Ye, Sensen Gao et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz, Frontier Labs); the paper's target metric, reported as recorded token traffic.

  • Bincheng Gu, Min Gao, Zongwei Wang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-10) cross-source; reported to improve alongside accuracy.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-07) tokens per task as the gap between waves.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-05) the one dimension with mixed reports; Zvi suspects a bug and effort settings.

  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-18) the gap between intelligence rank and verbosity rank, which move independently.

Related terms

metric

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.

20 linksconcept

Reasoning tokens

The internal deliberation tokens a model spends before answering; Tunguz quantifies how a smaller model spends more of them to close the gap with a larger one.

6 linksmetric

Compute efficiency

Capability delivered per unit of compute, combining model-side gains (active parameters, cost reductions) and chip-side gains; Tunguz's second profit lever alongside pricing.

6 linkstechnique

Mixture of experts

An architecture that activates only a fraction of parameters per token (GLM 5.3 Flash: 320B total, 18B active), the mechanism behind cheap capable open models.

7 linksconcept

Agentic inference workloads

The production traffic pattern created by coding and computer-use agents: many turns, context accumulating toward a million tokens, very high prefix reuse and bursty sub-agent spawns.

needed by 1 · 16 linksconcept

Cache pricing

The price charged for reading previously cached prompt tokens, which Anthropic cut 75 percent to $0.25 per million tokens with Fable 5.1 while leaving the headline per-token prices unchanged.
