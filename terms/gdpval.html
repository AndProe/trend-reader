GDPval

first seen 2026-W33 · 9 links · 1 min read

OpenAI's benchmark for real-world knowledge work, reported as an Elo score and used across Berman's release reviews as the knowledge-work counterpart to a coding score. Berman weights it and Terminal-Bench as the two numbers he reads off a release table, citing Grok 4.6's top score in August and Opus 5.5's 1,846 Elo against 1,735 for Fable 5.1 in September.

Also seen as GDPval 2.1

How the sources use it

  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-22) OpenAI's Elo benchmark for computer-based knowledge work.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-13) OpenAI's benchmark for real-world knowledge-work task performance, cited as Grok 4.6's strongest relative result. Established benchmark, recurring across Berman's videos.
