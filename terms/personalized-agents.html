Personalized agents

first seen 2026-W36 · 9 links · 1 min read

Agents that adapt to one person's preferences, history and situation rather than behaving identically for every user. The arXiv line reaches them through test-time adaptation from human interaction (TAHI) and through long-term memory models that keep a user's facts and preferences across sessions.

Also seen as personalised agents, user-adapted agents

How the sources use it

  • Cai Ke, Xinghao Chen, Xiaoyu Shen et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) recurring mention (arXiv: TAHI); agents adapting to an individual user's preferences.
  • Zora Zhiruo Wang, Apurva Gandhi, Rulin Shao et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-03) the target.
