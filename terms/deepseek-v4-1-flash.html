DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

first seen 2026-W37 · 5 links · 1 min read

DeepSeek's open-weights release, which Berman reads as benchmarking level with Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol and as his evidence that open weights reach the previous frontier generation about six months behind it. SemiAnalysis treats the same model as the reference Engram implementation, measuring its roughly 189 GiB memory table and reading its gate scan as memory optimising the training objective rather than selecting facts worth storing.

Also seen as DeepSeek V4.1, V4.1 Flash

Related terms

concept

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.

needed by 11 · 36 linksconcept

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.

15 linkstechnique

Mixture of experts

An architecture that activates only a fraction of parameters per token (GLM 5.3 Flash: 320B total, 18B active), the mechanism behind cheap capable open models.

7 linkstechnique

KV cache

The transformer's per-token memory that grows with context; capacity limits long-context serving on SRAM machines, reuse across turns is the systems problem agentic serving turns on, and test-time training is proposed as a fixed-size alternative.

needed by 2 · 12 linkstechnique

Quantization

Reducing numeric precision of weights or activations after training (INT8, NF4, NVFP4) to cut memory and energy; treated in the sustainability paper as a systems choice rather than a monotonic trade-off.
