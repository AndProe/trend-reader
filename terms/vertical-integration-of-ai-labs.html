Vertical integration of AI labs

first seen 2026-W20 · 8 links · 1 min read

Frontier labs extending from models into the layers around them, buying developer toolchains (Anthropic and Bun, OpenAI and Astral), owning coding harnesses and cutting off third-party platforms, so that the product, not the model, is where they compete; the counter-position to model-agnostic architecture.

Also seen as lab verticalisation, labs owning the stack

How the sources use it

  • Paolo Perrone (Technically, 2026-09-15) here applied to cloud providers owning chips through distribution rather than labs buying toolchains. Recurring and cross-source (Berman, Latent Space, Stratechery).
  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-07) recommendations follow lab product ownership (Trend Reader inference from the bias table).
  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-04) the health vertical as the example of OpenAI moving into products and services near users. Cross-source (Berman, Technically).
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-01) labs will verticalise around their own products after the Cursor cut-off, and model-agnostic platforms will fade.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-05-14) labs buying the toolchain layer beneath their coding products; Berman expects the same move at the harness layer.
