Enterprise Frontier Safeguards

first seen 2026-W36 · 4 links · 1 min read

Anthropic's enterprise data arrangement launched with Fable 5.1, under which customer data is stored in customer-controlled cloud infrastructure for misuse detection while Anthropic retains the ability to read it, replacing the retention policy that drew a backlash earlier in the year. Berman calls it a half measure that many enterprises may not accept; Thompson reads the retention walk-back as the market speaking under competitive pressure from OpenAI.

How the sources use it

  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-04) the title of the paid Update on Fable 5.1, whose description says the retention policy is not just altered but entirely removed in the meantime.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-01) customer-hosted data that Anthropic can still inspect; a half measure many enterprises may not accept.
