Enterprise AI adoption

first seen 2026-W18 · needed by 2 · 15 links · 3 min read

How companies actually take up AI: the gap between high enterprise agent token share and near-zero individual adoption, coding as the beachhead, shadow use, and methods like READY for deciding whether an agent is ready to deploy.

Also seen as enterprise adoption, agentic adoption gap, coding as AI beachhead

Related terms

technique

Deployment qualification

Statistically qualifying an agent together with its oversight policy against a reliability target before deployment (READY), or a constrained-success metric that counts only acceptable completions; the answer to benchmarks that do not predict production.

10 linksconcept

Shadow AI

Unsanctioned employee use of personal AI tools at work (bring your own AI); Berman's essay reframes it as an enablement failure to be solved with accountable agents rather than bans.

5 linksmetric

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.

20 linksconcept

Enterprise data governance

The compliance regime (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, EU processing rules, financial and patient records) that decides what may leave the company's machines and on what terms, forcing enterprises onto the expensive tier and framing the ban-or-trust choice.

needed by 2 · 11 linkstechnique

Smart model routing

Sending each request to the cheapest model that can handle it, frontier reserved for hard cases; Ramp's router, OpenRouter's marketplace and the mechanism behind local-model cost ceilings.

11 linksmetric

Enterprise agent tokens

The share of enterprise customers' tokens going to agentic tools rather than chat, reported by OpenAI at 64 percent for Codex against ChatGPT by June 2026.
