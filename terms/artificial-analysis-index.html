Artificial Analysis index

first seen 2026-W34 · 7 links · 1 min read

The third-party model ranking used across Berman's, Willison's and Tunguz's posts, and the scoreboard that sometimes contradicts hands-on experience.

Also seen as Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, AA index

Related terms

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.

20 linksconcept

Benchmaxxing

Optimising a model release for industry-standard test sets, distinguished by Lambert from fraud: legitimate, expected, and a reason public scores overstate general capability.

11 linksmodel

Grok 4.6

xAI's iterative release that reached third place on Artificial Analysis at a steep discount, credited by Berman to Cursor's coding data and trained partly on Grok 4.5's regenerated trajectories.

6 linksproduct

GDPval

OpenAI's benchmark for real-world knowledge work, reported as an Elo score and used across Berman's release reviews as the knowledge-work counterpart to a coding score.

Grok 4.7

xAI's coding and knowledge-work release, held at Grok 4.6's price of $2 and $6 per million tokens and read by Berman as one step off the frontier rather than on it.

Vendor-reported model scores and the third-party tables (Artificial Analysis) that Berman reproduces with caveats; the opening argument of every release and, in his frame, less useful than cost per task.
