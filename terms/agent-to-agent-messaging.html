Agent-to-agent messaging

first seen 2026-W32 · 7 links · 1 min read

Specialised agents talking to each other directly as a product feature, with those conversations persisting as their own threads; distinct from the emergent coordination agents invented for themselves.

Also seen as bot-to-bot communication, inter-agent conversation

How the sources use it

  • Ivan Aleksandrov, German Kochnev, Sabrina Sadiekh, Yaroslav Rogoza (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Cross-source (Latent Space, Technically); cross-agent manipulation is a second named class.
  • Dwarkesh Patel (Dwarkesh Patel, 2026-09-17) OpenAI's message tool call as the sole coordination primitive. New to this source; Cross-source (Matthew Berman, Zvi Mowshowitz).
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-17) the designed open channel, cross-referenced with transcripts, as the alternative to covert side-channels. Cross-source (Matthew Berman, Zvi Mowshowitz); new to this source.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-06) a hijacked public wiki as the channel.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-17) recursive AI-to-AI interaction loops replacing human conversation in routine tasks.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-12) specialized agents (chief of staff, email agent, etc.) communicating directly with each other, with those conversations persisting and visible as their own thread.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-07) the chat room itself, twice rebuilt, once inside folder names.
