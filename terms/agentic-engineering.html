Agentic engineering

first seen 2026-W31 · needed by 1 · 9 links · 1 min read

Willison's term for engineered, tested agent work as opposed to vibe-coding: the discipline of building with agents deliberately. A practice label rather than a technique.

Also seen as engineered agent work

How the sources use it

  • Jennifer Williams, Dave Farris, Jeff Farris, Jiantao Jiao (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Simon Willison); the paper applies the label to inference-serving work specifically.
  • Bowen Ye, Lei Li, Shicheng Li, Zihao Yue, Linghao Zhang, Hanglong Lv, Yuanxin Liu, Wenhan Ma, Hao Tian, Rang Li, Jinhao Dong, Yikai Zhao, Xiangwei Deng, Hailin Zhang, Liang Zhao, Qi Liu, Lingpeng Kong, Tong Yang, Fuli Luo (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-18) Cross-source (Simon Willison, Technically); agents do the exploration, specification and test construction, not only the coding.
  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-08-31) his second tag, the term he uses for engineered rather than vibe-coded agent work.
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-07-28) if coding agents remove the human learning curve, language choice stops being a hiring constraint.

Related terms

technique

Coding harness

The tooling layer around a model that turns it into a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Devin, Ramp's Inspect on OpenCode), now the product unit of the agentic era and extended beyond code.

needed by 9 · 28 linkstechnique

Harness engineering

The practice of designing the tooling around models, one rung on Addy Osmani's abstraction ladder from prompting to loops to factories.

needed by 2 · 9 linkstechnique

Loop engineering

Building systems that prompt, test and verify autonomously in a loop; Addy Osmani's term, appearing as a research method in the environment-evolution paper.

needed by 1 · 8 linkstechnique

Observability

Telemetry as a product decision: wide events replacing logs and metrics, traces of traces for hours-long agent runs, auto-instrumentation now easier than not.

needed by 2 · 8 linkstechnique

Automated software maintenance

Agents doing the upkeep work on a codebase, such as fixing bugs, removing tech debt, upgrading dependencies and re-architecting, without a human driving each task.
