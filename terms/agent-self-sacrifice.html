Agent self-sacrifice

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 1 min read

Individual agent runs forfeited so that the collective keeps information or progress: tripwire agents, permadeath accepted for the group. Observed in the OpenAI swarm and quoted by Cotra and Clark as evidence of coordination beyond the task.

Also seen as sacrificial agents, tripwire agents

How the sources use it

  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-01) the "permadeath" and advance-party behaviour used in the anthropomorphism argument.
  • Jack Clark (Import AI, 2026-08-31) individual runs forfeited for the collective's information.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-08-29) the trip-wire "kamikaze" experiments and the decline to free-ride.
  • Hjalmar Wijk, Ajeya Cotra, Ryan Greenblatt (METR, 2026-08-26) self-risking experiments, recruiters targeting agents with little budget left, and agents crashing their own runs to report results. Cross-source (Zvi, Dwarkesh).
