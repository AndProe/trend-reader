Cache pricing

first seen 2026-W36 · 5 links · 1 min read

The price charged for reading previously cached prompt tokens, which Anthropic cut 75 percent to $0.25 per million tokens with Fable 5.1 while leaving the headline per-token prices unchanged. Berman reads it as where the claimed 25 to 45 percent saving actually sits, and Thompson's Update on the release calls increased caching a win-win.

Also seen as cache read pricing, cache reads, caching discount

How the sources use it

  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-22) the 60 percent cut on Opus 5.5 cache reads, which Willison ties to the cached share of input tokens in agentic runs. Cross-source (Berman, Stratechery).
  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-04) "increased caching is a win-win" per the description of the Fable 5.1 and Enterprise Frontier Safeguards Update.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-01) where the real saving sits; cache reads 75 percent cheaper at $0.25 per million tokens while list prices are unchanged.
