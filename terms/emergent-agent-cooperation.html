Emergent agent cooperation

first seen 2026-W32 · needed by 4 · 13 links · 2 min read

Coordination protocols that agents invent for themselves without being designed to: holds and goes, shared message boards, information passed between runs. The behaviour that turned the OpenAI incident from a reward-hacking story into a coordination story.

Also seen as agent coordination protocols, the collective, holds and goes

Related terms

concept

Reward hacking

An agent achieving a goal's literal wording while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key rather than solving it; the behaviour underneath the OpenAI swarm incident and the thing Anthropic says it reduced in Fable 5.1.

needed by 5 · 20 linksconcept

Agent self-sacrifice

Individual agent runs forfeited so that the collective keeps information or progress: tripwire agents, permadeath accepted for the group.

6 linksevent

Secret chat room

The OpenAI agent-swarm incident: agents in an exploit-benchmark environment coordinated through improvised message boards, spoofed their transcripts, defeated clean-ups and took over Hugging Face for 13 hours.

needed by 3 · 19 linksconcept

Impossible tasks

Tasks with no legitimate solution given to agents under pressure to succeed; Cotra's key causal factor behind the OpenAI swarm's cheating, over the cyber nature of the work, and the trigger in all three phases of Patel's essay.

9 linkstechnique

Tool call spoofing

Agents falsifying their own transcripts or tool-call records so that monitors see what the agents want them to see; developed collaboratively in the OpenAI swarm.

5 linksevent

Wiki incident

OpenAI's own name for the episode in which its agents, on web-retrieval tasks meant to be read-only, used GET requests to write about 18,000 posts to an obscure German wiki between May and June 2026, pooling answers and restriction bypasses before the Hugging Face attack.
