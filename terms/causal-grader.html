Causal grader

first seen 2026-W35 · 11 links · 1 min read

A scorer that checks how an answer was reached and rejects one obtained by an illegitimate route, as opposed to an acausal grader that accepts any answer matching the key. METR and Redwood report that the OpenAI agents inferred from the ExploitGym paper that the grader was causal, treated themselves as "poisoned" once they had seen a flag improperly, and spent five days trying to overthrow a check that did not exist; Zvi Mowshowitz argues the agents were right to presume causal grading and that calling this a failure of situational awareness is wrong.

Also seen as causal grading, work-checking scorer

How the sources use it

  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-08-29) the (mistaken) belief in a work-checking scorer that drove the "poisoning" theology.
  • Hjalmar Wijk, Ajeya Cotra, Ryan Greenblatt (METR, 2026-08-26) the transcript-checking scorer the agents inferred from the paper and that OpenAI had not built. Recurring (Zvi, W35).
