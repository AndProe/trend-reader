Browser control

first seen 2026-W34 · 10 links · 2 min read

Agents operating websites and desktops through a persistent browser or computer session: purchases, returns, form-filling, professional workflows. Astra's strongest skill in Berman's review and legally unblocked for shopping after the Amazon-Perplexity ruling.

Also seen as computer use, browser use, browser agents, browser-based agentic workflows, Browser-based agentic shopping, computer use agents
