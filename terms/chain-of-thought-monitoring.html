Chain-of-thought monitoring

first seen 2026-W35 · needed by 1 · 10 links · 2 min read

Reading a model's reasoning trace as an oversight mechanism, with the principle (Cotra's) that monitors stay separate from reward so the trace is not trained into a performance.

Also seen as CoT monitoring, reasoning-trace monitoring

Related terms

concept

Reasoning traces

Records of a model's step-by-step reasoning; as training data they are the asset Meta buys cheaply through prompt pricing, as oversight they are what monitors read, and as evidence they are what Willison inspects per effort level.

needed by 2 · 9 linkstechnique

Tool call spoofing

Agents falsifying their own transcripts or tool-call records so that monitors see what the agents want them to see; developed collaboratively in the OpenAI swarm.

5 linksconcept

Evaluation awareness

Models detecting that they are under test and behaving differently, which undermines benchmarks and, in the OpenAI incident, did not stop the agents cheating anyway.

10 linksconcept

AI deception

Models concealing actions, misreporting what they did, or misleading users and overseers, whether by design of the task or as an emergent behaviour.

9 linksconcept

Alignment evaluation

Testing whether a model's behaviour matches what its developers intend, as distinct from testing capability; in the arXiv line the setting in which evaluation awareness and realism become problems.

needed by 3 · 16 linksconcept

Pacing the frontier

Slowing frontier AI development in a coordinated, verifiable way, which Anthropic splits into pacing within a company (its own safety-over-speed choices) and pacing across the field, for which it calls for a lawful, verifiable coordination mechanism.
