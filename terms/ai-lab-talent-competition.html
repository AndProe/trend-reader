AI lab talent competition

first seen 2026-W32 · 5 links · 1 min read

Frontier labs and AI startups out-hiring Big Tech, trading firms and each other for engineers and researchers, and the departures that follow. Seen in Meta's exodus, Optiver's recruiting losses and the DeepMind researchers leaving to found neolabs.

Also seen as talent war, lab hiring competition

How the sources use it

  • Vinoo Ganesh (Latent Space, 2026-09-12) labs, startups and PE firms hiring FDEs; he says every lab bids for the same few hundred people.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-20) labs and AI startups out-hiring Big Tech for engineers.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-11) Anthropic and OpenAI recruiting from prop shops.
