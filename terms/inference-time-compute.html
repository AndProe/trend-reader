Inference-time compute

first seen 2026-W32 · needed by 4 · 15 links · 2 min read

Spending more computation at answer time (longer reasoning, more candidates, verification) rather than in training; OpenAI's strategic bet in Lambert's reading and the cost the arXiv verification papers try to reduce.

Also seen as test-time compute, inference scaling

How the sources use it

  • Richard MacManus (Latent Space, 2026-09-25) the whole real-time problem restated as a budget: frames must be generated one at a time and fast enough to play.
  • RJ Honicky (Latent Space, 2026-09-23) the aptamer result read as chain-of-thought in the DNA language: the model continues a ranked trajectory past the scores it was shown.
  • Trang Nguyen, Eulrang Cho, Bingqing Chen, Tim Dettmers (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Dwarkesh Patel, Interconnects, Simon Willison); the reported gain is on the cost axis of test-time scaling, including the parallel variant.
  • Wenbo Pan, Zhichao Liu, Shujie Liu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Dwarkesh Patel, Interconnects, Simon Willison); the authors report that raising the reasoning budget leaves fork accuracy unchanged.
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-09-19) mass inference capacity as the near-term accelerant, which he separates from RSI. Recurring;.
  • Jaejun Shim, HyunJin Kim, Young Jin Kim et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Interconnects, Simon Willison); the paper treats how much to spend at answer time as something the model should decide per instance.
  • Dwarkesh Patel (Dwarkesh Patel, 2026-09-17) multi-agent as parallel test-time compute with a sublinear speed-up. New to this source; Cross-source (Interconnects, arXiv cs.AI, Frontier Labs).
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-15) the Extended Thinking variant spends more reasoning while keeping the conversation live. Cross-source (Interconnects, Simon Willison).
  • Bincheng Gu, Min Gao, Zongwei Wang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-10) cross-source; a frozen-model, prefix-only method applied at inference.
  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-08) 300 billion output tokens across one search effort.
  • Axel Ahlqvist (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-02) spent on the evaluator, not the model under test. Existing concept (Tunguz's test-time posts).
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-01) extra reasoning and tool calls at answer time in exchange for fewer input tokens. Cross-source (Interconnects, arXiv).
  • Xianzhi Li (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-08-31) the cost being reduced. Existing concept.
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-08-09) arXiv's term; here as OpenAI's strategic bet.

Related terms

concept

Reasoning tokens

The internal deliberation tokens a model spends before answering; Tunguz quantifies how a smaller model spends more of them to close the gap with a larger one.

6 linksconcept

Reasoning effort levels

The effort dial (low to max) that sets how much a model thinks per request, with a 33x cost range on one prompt in Willison's test and two settings apparently identical.

9 linkstechnique

Self-verification

An agent or model checking its own work: closing the loop with tests, telemetry, flags and screenshots at Ramp, or selecting among candidate solutions with a hidden-state reward model at inference.

6 linkstechnique

Test-time training

A model updating its weights on the prompt it is answering rather than staying frozen, replacing the linearly growing KV cache with a fixed-size update; Tunguz's 'When Models Learn'.

needed by 1 · 8 linksmetric

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.

23 linkstechnique

Recurrent depth

A looped-transformer technique, reported by The Information to be used in GPT-6 Astra, that repeats computation through the same layers so that part of a model's reasoning happens inside the network rather than in its written chain of thought.
