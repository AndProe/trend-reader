Cognitive debt

first seen 2026-W34 · 6 links · 1 min read

The loss of memory, understanding and judgement that accumulates when people accept model output as their own without engaging with it, and the organisational version when experienced engineers are removed without notice. Addy Osmani's term, with cognitive surrender as its individual act.

Also seen as cognitive surrender, domain knowledge loss, skill atrophy

How the sources use it

  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-10) engineers losing system knowledge when AI handles incidents.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-03) the cost of removing experienced engineers without notice.
  • Ethan Mollick (Ethan Mollick, 2026-08-31) if agents take the interesting decisions, people stop developing judgement, worsening the training of new experts.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-19) loss of memory and understanding from over-relying on AI.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-19) accepting the model's answer as your own without judgment.
