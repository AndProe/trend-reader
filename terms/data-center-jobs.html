Data center jobs

first seen 2026-W36 · 5 links · 1 min read

Employment created by the AI buildout in data-center construction, power infrastructure and skilled trades, set against jobs lost to AI. The Forward Future team reports trade unions threatening candidates who slow data-center projects, and relays The Economist's estimate of around 1 million jobs created against roughly 200,000 announced AI-related layoffs.
