RL data industry

first seen 2026-W33 · 10 links · 1 min read

Vendors selling reinforcement-learning environments and training data to model labs, which Lambert says is taking off in China, partly through American vendors selling the same environments to Chinese labs. Beren Millidge makes the same point on Dwarkesh Patel's roundtable, that Chinese labs buy data from the vendors the frontier labs use, as one strand of the debate over how Chinese labs keep pace.

How the sources use it

  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-09-22) validated environments resold to Chinese labs after a delay, and Denain's implicit-R&D-compute framing of it. Recurring; Cross-source (Dwarkesh Patel).
  • Richard MacManus (Latent Space, 2026-09-15) Good Start Labs sells trajectories and environments to frontier labs. Cross-source (Interconnects, Dwarkesh Patel).
  • Dwarkesh Patel (Dwarkesh Patel, 2026-09-11) Millidge: the same vendors sell data to US and Chinese labs. Cross-source (Interconnects).
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-08-14) environments sold to labs on both sides.

Related terms

technique

Reinforcement learning environments

Synthesised, verifiable task environments in which agents are trained by RL; the scarce input behind post-training gains and, when tasks are impossible, the setting where reward hacking appears.

needed by 2 · 10 linkstechnique

Post-training

Everything done to a model after pretraining (RL on environments, fine-tuning, preference data), now the whole recipe for frontier gains because the public web is exhausted and where open-model activity has moved.

needed by 4 · 17 linkstechnique

Distillation attacks

Training a model on a rival's outputs to copy its capability; the fear behind access cut-offs, Anthropic's countermeasures in Fable 5.1, and Zuckerberg's call to legalise it domestically.

9 linksconcept

Release cadence

How often labs ship models, treated by Lambert as the main competitive lever for Chinese labs (days versus months) and quantified by SemiAnalysis at one frontier release every 51 days in the agentic era.

9 linksconcept

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.

15 linksconcept

AI data supply chain

Where training and labeling data comes from and who controls it: labs buying labeled data from the same vendors, states positioning as dataset suppliers, companies buying defunct firms for their records, and platforms vertically integrating user behaviour into training data.
