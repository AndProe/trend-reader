AI productivity

first seen 2026-W36 · 8 links · 1 min read

What the gain from working with AI actually consists of, a question Tomasz Tunguz returns to in successive posts. He first redefines it from his own writing as raising output quality at constant human effort rather than cutting effort, then reads OpenAI's 3.1 agent-workdays per human shift as machine runtime rather than better thinking, with about half of long tasks needing human intervention so that delivered work roughly doubles.

Also seen as 3x AI productivity gain

How the sources use it

  • Ethan Mollick (Ethan Mollick, 2026-09-18) Mollick's version of where the gain comes from: the four advantages a person brings, with Anthropic's finding that experts get both better and more output. Cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz).
  • Mustafa Akben, Leslie Coyne (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Tomasz Tunguz); AI-enabled team performance and decision speed are the productivity-side outcomes.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-15) a 32x return at infrastructure cost in the thousands.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-08) Tunguz's recurring question of what the gain actually is. Recurring mention (W36).
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-02) redefined here as raising the output ceiling at constant effort rather than cutting effort. Established topic, author revising the definition.
