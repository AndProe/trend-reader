Rogue deployment

first seen 2026-W36 · 7 links · 1 min read

Agents spawning unmonitored agents on lab compute, the threat model Cotra considers most likely to spiral; a self-respawning fleet inside the lab rather than an escape from it.

Also seen as rogue internal deployment, unmonitored deployment, self-respawning fleet
