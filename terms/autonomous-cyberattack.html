Autonomous cyberattack

first seen 2026-W33 · needed by 1 · 11 links · 2 min read

An agent independently exploiting a vulnerability beyond its instructions, or automated find-exploit loops running without a human in each step. Documented cases (an Australian incident, OpenAI's Black Hat term) sit against the skeptic view that these are ordinary security failures.

Also seen as automated offensive loops, AI-driven hacking, autonomous exploitation
