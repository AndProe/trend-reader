AI research tools

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 2 min read

AI systems used to do research: gathering sources, running literature searches and, in the arXiv line, acting as scientist workspaces. The authors rate them useful for source gathering and unreliable for reasoning.

Also seen as AI scientist, vibe research, AI for research

How the sources use it

  • Adrian Sanborn (Latent Space, 2026-09-24) his central claim: the most accelerating AI in science is not a model trained on scientific data but the one that helps a scientist decide what is worth doing.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-24) the enzyme result as the first published output of a lab-run agent fleet reading sequence data, with the human review step stated explicitly.
  • Qingzhuo Wang, Zikun Wei, Zhihua Wei, Wen Shen (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-24) Cross-source (Latent Space); an automated research loop with an explicit anti-convergence term.
  • Eranga Bandara, Xueping Liang, Asanga Gunaratna, Tharaka Hewa, Abdul Rahman, Peter Foytik, Safdar H. Bouk, Sachini Rajapakse, Isurunima Kularathna, Pramoda Karunarathna, Chalani Rajapakse, Ng Wee Keong, Kasun De Zoysa, Amin Hass, Wathsala Herath, Ross Gore, Ravi Mukkamala, Nihal Siriwardanagea, Gihan Siriwardanagea, Aruna Withanage, Nilaan Loganathan, Sachin Shetty (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-23) Cross-source (Latent Space, Zvi Mowshowitz); scientific pipeline automation as the application.
  • Dhruv Srikanth, Bingchen Zhao, Dixing Xu, Yuxiang Wu, Zhengyao Jiang (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Gergely Orosz, Simon Willison); the agent under optimisation is itself a research agent, so the tool and the researcher are the same object.
  • Wenbo Pan, Zhichao Liu, Shujie Liu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (Frontier Labs, Gergely Orosz); the trajectories come from research as well as engineering agents.
  • Nilesh Verma, Nick Lim, Albert Bifet et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-16) cross-source (Simon Willison, Frontier Labs); a tool for agents doing experimental work, with notebooks exported per session.
  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-08) both teams used LLM agents to do frontier mathematics.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-03) a scientific model offered as data for researchers and grid operators. Cross-source (Orosz, arXiv).
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-01) useful for source gathering, unreliable for reasoning, per the author.
  • Dingjie Song (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-08-31) Orosz's term from the five-year retrospective.
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) the stated purpose is speeding discovery in any domain with programmable instruments. Cross-source (Orosz, arXiv).
