Generative video

first seen 2026-W35 · 10 links · 1 min read

Models that synthesise video, and increasingly synchronised audio, rather than editing or compositing existing footage. Runway's engineers describe the shift that matters as generating frame by frame fast enough to be interactive, which requires converting bidirectional diffusion into an autoregressive model and then distilling it; Germanidis draws the line to a world model at counterfactual generation, meaning equally realistic outcomes for the action the user did not take.

Also seen as video generation, text-to-video

How the sources use it

  • Kamil Sindi, Robin Kahlow and Anastasis Germanidis (Latent Space, 2026-09-25) the field they are moving from clip generation to real-time interactive simulation, with error accumulation as the central problem of the autoregressive form.
  • Richard MacManus (Latent Space, 2026-09-25) the category the episode is about, and the line Germanidis draws inside it: counterfactual generation separates a world model from a video model.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) the capability being exposed with more control in a Gemini Omni release.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-27) the product category, absent from the vault so far.
