Vendor financing

first seen 2026-W32 · 9 links · 1 min read

Nvidia extending its balance sheet to sustain demand for its own chips: investing in labs and neoclouds, financing data centres directly, financing SpaceX's buildout. Called circular financing by critics and compared to the dot-com era.

Also seen as NVIDIA financing, circular financing, customer financing

  • Daniel Nishball, Oliver Kennon, Terence Ong (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-11) the disclosed size of Nvidia financing its own demand. Recurring (SemiAnalysis W32) and cross-source.

  • Gaurav Sharma (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-01) NVIDIA funding its own demand; the same debate The Economist raises.

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-19) NVIDIA extending its own balance sheet to sustain AI lab demand, now including direct data-center financing.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-07) Berman's and Tunguz's term; Nvidia financing SpaceX.
