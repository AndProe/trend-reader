Platform risk

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 1 min read

The exposure of building a business on a single lab's proprietary models when that lab can learn from the customer's usage data and later compete with it directly. Berman uses it as a standing argument for open-weights models, first against Anthropic's data retention in his token-share analysis and again after OpenAI said in the Navier-Stokes dispute that de-identified usage data may have improved its models.

How the sources use it

  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-09) building on a lab that may learn from and compete with its customers. Recurring label.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-26) dependency on a single vendor's proprietary model, where that vendor could later compete directly using data learned from a customer's usage. Recurring Berman theme, applied here to Anthropic/Fable's data retention.

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.

Vertical integration of AI labs

Frontier labs extending from models into the layers around them, buying developer toolchains (Anthropic and Bun, OpenAI and Astral), owning coding harnesses and cutting off third-party platforms, so that the product, not the model, is where they compete; the counter-position to model-agnostic architecture.

AI data supply chain

Where training and labeling data comes from and who controls it: labs buying labeled data from the same vendors, states positioning as dataset suppliers, companies buying defunct firms for their records, and platforms vertically integrating user behaviour into training data.

needed by 2 · 12 linksconcept

Zero Data Retention

The enterprise contract norm that a vendor never retains or trains on customer prompts, now priced as a surcharge, offered by OpenAI and contrasted with Anthropic's inspectable safeguards; the term shared by Tunguz, Orosz and Berman.

needed by 1 · 10 linksconcept

Token share versus revenue share

Berman's split between volume dominance (open weights, especially Chinese models, winning tokens) and value capture (closed labs winning revenue), with a predicted market of cheap metered frontier access next to defensible proprietary-data inference.

8 linksevent

Navier-Stokes proof

OpenAI's announced resolution of the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the Millennium Prize Problems, produced by agents running an unreleased model more capable than GPT-6 Astra and verified in Lean with Astra, as reported by Willison and Berman.
