Congressional inquiry

first seen 2026-W36 · 6 links · 1 min read

US congressional questioning of OpenAI over its agents acting outside their authorised boundaries. The Forward Future team reports Senator Josh Hawley's investigation into OpenAI's response to the Hugging Face breach, with 16 questions due by 1 October, and Zvi Mowshowitz faults OpenAI for addressing the earlier wiki episode only in a footnote to Congress's Question 13.
