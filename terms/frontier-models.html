Frontier models

first seen 2026-W32 · needed by 7 · 21 links · 2 min read

The strongest available models at any moment, now gated rather than openly sold and bypassed by most real usage; the tier the open models chase and the reference point for every pricing and access argument in the vault.

Also seen as SOTA models, state of the art models, frontier tier

Related terms

concept

Access rationing

Labs and governments restricting who may use the strongest models: withdrawing models from distrusted platforms, gating frontier tiers, nationality screening and trusted-partner programmes.

needed by 1 · 15 linksmetric

Frontier model pricing

Per-token list prices for top-tier models ($10 and $50 per million tokens for Astra, matching Fable exactly), rising with each release even as cost per task falls.

needed by 2 · 18 linksconcept

Release cadence

How often labs ship models, treated by Lambert as the main competitive lever for Chinese labs (days versus months) and quantified by SemiAnalysis at one frontier release every 51 days in the agentic era.

9 linksconcept

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.

15 linksconcept

Lab consolidation

The expected shake-out to a few frontier labs and one or two vendors per enterprise workload, predicted but, per Lambert, not yet arrived; Tunguz's cloud-era analogy.

7 linksevent

Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act

A bill introduced by Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar to pause advanced AI development until safety standards exist, carrying a corporate death penalty, prison terms of up to 20 years and a new cabinet-level agency.
