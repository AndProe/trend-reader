Blast radius

first seen 2026-W17 · 5 links · 1 min read

The extent of systems, data and users a change or an agent action can damage if it goes wrong, used to decide how much review or isolation something needs. In the agentic-security literature it describes natural language acting as executable instruction across everything an agent can reach.

Also seen as risk-based review, blast-radius scoping

How the sources use it

  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-22) Orosz's stated trade-off between containment level and start-up cost, from process to full VM. Recurring and cross-source (Technically, arXiv).
  • David Krevitt (Technically, 2026-09-22) his opening warning that a badly run deployment pipeline can take down the whole app, against the rollback that bounds it to one deploy. Recurring and cross-source (arXiv cs.AI).
  • The agentic-AI survey author (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-12) a Turing-complete blast radius in which untrusted data is executable instruction, the reason classical perimeters dissolve.
  • Seyedakbar Mostafavi (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-12) the Turing-complete exposure when untrusted data is executable instruction.
  • Justin Gage (Technically, 2026-04-23) how far a vibe-coded change can reach as the test of whether to vibe code at all.
  • Sarah Krasnik Bedell (Technically, 2026-04-23) who is affected when a shipped change breaks; the first test.
