Vera Rubin

first seen 2026-W32 · 5 links · 1 min read

Nvidia's next-generation GPU platform (Rubin, Rubin Ultra), shipping with HBM4, 5.4x the performance per megawatt of GB200, reportedly redesigned with less memory; the right comparator for Jalapeño.

Also seen as Rubin, Rubin Ultra, Vera Rubin NVL72

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Tanj Bennett, Bryan Shan (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-21) named as strong at the arithmetic-intense midfill layer and less effective at memory-intense decode.
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Cam Quilici, Alec Ibarra, Kimbo Chen, Myron Xie (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-18) the Rubin Ultra HBM respec as the motive for architecture-side memory savings.
  • Dylan Patel, Bryan Shan, Alec Ibarra, Cam Quilici, Wenyao Gao (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-14) the subject: first agentic benchmark results for the NVL72 rack. Recurring and cross-source (Berman).
  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Harrison Barclay, Minjae Kang (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-13) Rubin Ultra's cut to 192GB per GPU and the NVL576 system used for the model. Recurring (SemiAnalysis W32) and cross-source.
  • Arya Tschand, Yaosheng Fu, Vikram Sharma Mailthody et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-11) cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Berman); the Rubin-plus-LPX system is the forward-looking hardware model.
  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-07) NVIDIA's next-generation GPU, reportedly being redesigned with less memory than planned.

Related terms

product

HBM

High Bandwidth Memory, whose production pivot starved conventional DRAM and SSD supply and whose quadrupled prices drive a CHIPS Act lobbying fight; SK Hynix's $720 billion bet.

5 linksmetric

Performance per watt

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

11 linksconcept

Custom silicon

Accelerators designed by the companies that use them rather than bought from Nvidia: Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA, OpenAI's Jalapeño, Cerebras wafers for fast tiers.

needed by 2 · 9 linksconcept

CUDA moat

Nvidia's software advantage: the CUDA stack and the day-0 framework support that make its GPUs the default target for every serving engine, which competitors must match before their hardware counts.

7 linksproduct

Jalapeno chip

OpenAI's Broadcom-built inference chip, designed from mid-2024 and taped out in 16 months, beating Blackwell on tokens per megawatt with single-token prediction; Rubin is the fair comparator and the results are OpenAI's own.
