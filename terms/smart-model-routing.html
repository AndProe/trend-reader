Smart model routing

first seen 2026-W16 · 12 links · 2 min read

Sending each request to the cheapest model that can handle it, frontier reserved for hard cases; Ramp's router, OpenRouter's marketplace and the mechanism behind local-model cost ceilings.

Also seen as model routing, Ramp Router, routing to open models

Related terms

concept

Open weights models

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era.

needed by 11 · 36 linksmetric

Cost per task

Dollars per completed task rather than per token or per benchmark point; Berman's preferred economic benchmark, applied per effort level by Willison and now adopted in the labs' own charts.

23 linksconcept

On-device inference

Running models locally on the user's machine or on-premise, now competitive with cloud frontier models for most tasks at the cost of thinking time, and eliminating per-token metering for always-on agents.

needed by 1 · 13 linksconcept

Price elasticity

How much demand responds to model price; Ramp's data show buyers do not pay premiums proportional to capability, the evidence behind Tunguz's 'who buys SOTA' argument.

4 linksconcept

Neo clouds

GPU-rental clouds outside the hyperscalers (CoreWeave-type providers) that now drive most net-new data-centre revenue, serve open models at competitive prices, and, per SemiAnalysis's audits, mostly run insecure software stacks.

8 linksmodel

Jev

TypeSafe AI's decision model, released 15 September 2026, which takes a state and a set of options and returns a typed, calibrated choice in one parallel pass instead of generating text.
