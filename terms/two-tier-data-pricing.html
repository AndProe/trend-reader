Two-tier data pricing

first seen 2026-W36 · 7 links · 1 min read

Selling the same model at two prices: a Standard tier on which the lab promises never to train on prompts and completions, and a Contributor tier about 92 to 95 percent cheaper in exchange for training rights, as Meta did with Muse Spark 1.3 at $1.25/$4.25 against $0.10/$0.20 per million tokens. Tunguz reads the spread as the market's first liquid price for prompt data and the advertising business model arriving in AI; Berman's daily records it as Meta's discount for training consent.

Also seen as Standard tier / Contributor tier, contributor pricing, data-for-access barter

How the sources use it

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-04) Meta's discount for training consent, about 95 percent cheaper for users who let prompts and outputs train future models; the same event Tunguz analysed.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-03) the same model sold at a private price and a training-consent price; the $1.24 per million token spread as the first liquid quote for prompt data and a privacy surcharge of about $454,000 a year at a billion tokens a day.
