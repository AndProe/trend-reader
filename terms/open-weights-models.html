Open weights models

first seen 2026-W16 · needed by 11 · 36 links · 5 min read

Models whose weights are published for download and self-hosting, now good enough for most production workloads and closing on the closed frontier in about half the time each era. The vault's most shared term, appearing as competitor, hedge, exploit tool and licensing question.

Also seen as open models, open source models, open-weight models

Related terms

concept

Open-closed capability gap

The measured distance between the best open and best closed models: 13 months to close in the scaling era, 8.5 in the reasoning era, about five in the agentic era.

15 linksconcept

Revenue-share licenses

Open-model licences that are free to experiment with and require a commercial agreement, security review or revenue share above a threshold (Kimi K3 at $20 million a year); 'open until you scale'.

7 linkstechnique

Distillation attacks

Training a model on a rival's outputs to copy its capability; the fear behind access cut-offs, Anthropic's countermeasures in Fable 5.1, and Zuckerberg's call to legalise it domestically.

9 linksconcept

Chinese AI chips

Domestically designed accelerators (Ascend and successors) and the co-designed chip, interconnect and serving stacks that now train and serve frontier-adjacent Chinese models at scale, shown by GLM 5.3 Flash serving 100 trillion tokens a day and LongCat trained end to end on Ascend.

8 linksconcept

Neo clouds

GPU-rental clouds outside the hyperscalers (CoreWeave-type providers) that now drive most net-new data-centre revenue, serve open models at competitive prices, and, per SemiAnalysis's audits, mostly run insecure software stacks.

8 linksconcept

Commoditise your complement

The strategy of giving away the thing next to what you sell so that demand for your product grows: Nvidia releasing open models and data to sell chips, Meta flooding the zone with tokens to hurt token-sellers.
