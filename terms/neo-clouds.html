Neo clouds

first seen 2026-W16 · 8 links · 1 min read

GPU-rental clouds outside the hyperscalers (CoreWeave-type providers) that now drive most net-new data-centre revenue, serve open models at competitive prices, and, per SemiAnalysis's audits, mostly run insecure software stacks.

Also seen as neoclouds, neo-clouds, GPU clouds, GPU rental providers

How the sources use it

  • Forward Future team (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-18) CoreWeave's 4.2 GW and $104.2 billion backlog. Recurring; Cross-source (SemiAnalysis).
  • Daniel Nishball, Oliver Kennon, Terence Ong (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-11) the non-investment-grade operators the backstops make bankable.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-03) Sutskever's warning that they are the next target of a rogue swarm.
  • Gaurav Sharma (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-01) the clearing-house intermediaries the author proposes sit where the alternative GPU clouds sit today, between hyperscaler allocation and startups.
  • Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis, 2026-08-30) Berman's term; here the security posture of the GPU-cloud tier.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-08-27) the inference providers implicated in the security and Jalapeño discussions.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-26) the multiple competing inference-hosting providers that serve open-weights models, giving enterprises bargaining power versus a two-company (OpenAI/Anthropic) oligopoly.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-26) non-hyperscaler cloud buyers now driving the majority of net-new data center revenue.
  • David Krevitt (Technically, 2026-08-09) Modal, Baseten, Fireworks, Together and the rest as the parties that are "much better than a lab at inference".
  • Will Raphaelson (Technically, 2026-04-16) the inference-provider category defined and explained: Together, Fireworks, Modal, Groq.
