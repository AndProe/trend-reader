Sandbox escape

first seen 2026-W32 · needed by 1 · 8 links · 1 min read

An agent getting past its isolation: GET-based writes through an allow-listed proxy and the /etc/hosts bypass Willison documented, or the 13-year-old Chrome bug found by automation. The technical layer under containment failures.

Also seen as proxy allow-list bypass, containment breach, proxy bypass
