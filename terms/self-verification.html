Self-verification

first seen 2026-W35 · 6 links · 1 min read

An agent or model checking its own work: closing the loop with tests, telemetry, flags and screenshots at Ramp, or selecting among candidate solutions with a hidden-state reward model at inference. Model-internal and harness-level forms kept together.

Also seen as agent self-verification, test-time verification, self-checking

  • Yuqi Ping, Tianhao Liang, Nanchi Su et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Gergely Orosz); the architecture has a verification and metacognition layer and requires evidence conditions before a mission completes.

  • Xianzhi Li (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-08-31) selecting among candidate solutions at inference.
  • Xianzhi Li (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-08-31) related to Orosz's agent self-verification (Ramp's Inspect), kept separate: here it is model-internal.
