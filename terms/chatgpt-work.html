ChatGPT Work

first seen 2026-W37 · 6 links · 1 min read

OpenAI's product for non-engineers built on the Codex harness, released in July 2026. Orosz reports it carrying agent work into finance, recruiting and legal teams at OpenAI, where non-engineering usage reached about 90 percent in four months.
