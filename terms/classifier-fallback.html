Classifier fallback

first seen 2026-W36 · 6 links · 1 min read

Anthropic's deployment mechanism in which a request that trips a safety classifier on Fable 5.1 is answered by an older fallback model, in practice Opus 4.8. Zvi reads it as the main practical complaint about Fable 5.1 and the locus of its remaining prompt-injection failures, since 21 of 29 successful browser attacks in the system card hit the fallback rather than the primary model.

Also seen as fallback model, fallback to Opus 4.8

How the sources use it

  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-21) the same pattern read as a safety property, the decider handing back what it will not decide. New for this source; cross-source (Zvi Mowshowitz).
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-05) the downgrade to Opus 4.8 when a classifier fires, the main practical complaint about Fable 5.1.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-04) 21 of 29 successful browser prompt-injection attacks hit the fallback model, 20 of them Opus 4.8; Zvi wants an option to disable the fallback or fall back to Opus 5.
