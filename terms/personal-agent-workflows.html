Personal agent workflows

first seen 2026-W34 · 15 links · 1 min read

Individuals using a single agent or chief-of-staff bot to triage email, summarise meetings, coordinate the day and delegate to sub-bots over chat; the consumer-side pattern Grok Bot and Orosz's inbox agent illustrate, and Sottiaux's rationale for a universal personal agent.

Also seen as chief of staff agent, AI inbox agent, email triage automation, personal AGI, daily coordinator pattern, Meeting summary workflow

How the sources use it

  • Alex Salkever (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-22) calendar and email access, alarms, location correction and unprompted suggestions.
  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-21) Muse as the product case: a personal agent holds a user's information and is harder to leave than a chatbot. Cross-source (Latent Space, Gergely Orosz).
  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-11) agents for ordinary people as the "AI upside" made accessible; the Mac Mini aside points at the self-hosted alternative. Cross-source (Latent Space, Berman, Orosz).
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-09) Sottiaux's mobile dictation workflow and "personal AGI" goal.
  • Nick Wentz (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-08) Dia's morning brief as the invisible-agent pattern.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-27) an agent triaging and replying to his email.
  • Matthew Berman; Tibo Sottiaux (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-24) Sottiaux's term for a single, universally adaptive agent interface (used by "you and your mom" alike) that tailors itself to each user's technical level and needs, the stated rationale behind merging Codex into ChatGPT.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-24) a 24-hour-lifetime agent that delegates to specialists and consolidates learnings nightly.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-20) a single designated bot used as the primary point of contact that delegates to specialized sub-bots, especially over Slack/Telegram.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-20) scheduled, context-aware email archiving, batching, and reply-drafting using connected Gmail/HubSpot/Drive data.
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-08-20) automated transcript ingestion (via Fathom), summarization, and action-item extraction delivered through chat.
