Energy efficiency

first seen 2026-W36 · 4 links · 1 min read

Measured GPU energy per unit of model work, treated as a first-class pillar in the triple-bottom-line sustainability paper; the research-side twin of performance per watt.

Also seen as energy per token, GPU energy

How the sources use it

  • Arya Tschand, Yaosheng Fu, Vikram Sharma Mailthody et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-11) recurring (arXiv); measured as tokens per joule.
  • Jainil Dharmil Shah (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-01) measured GPU energy as a first-class pillar; ties to Tunguz's intelligence-per-watt framing.

Performance per watt

Tokens or intelligence per unit of power, the metric OpenAI designs Jalapeño for and Tunguz's Koomey-style framing; the right unit when power, not money, is the binding constraint.

Quantization

Reducing numeric precision of weights or activations after training (INT8, NF4, NVFP4) to cut memory and energy; treated in the sustainability paper as a systems choice rather than a monotonic trade-off.

6 linksconcept

Small language models

Models designed small from the start rather than compressed from large ones, evaluated in the arXiv sustainability line for energy and quality per parameter.

7 linksmetric

Compute efficiency

Capability delivered per unit of compute, combining model-side gains (active parameters, cost reductions) and chip-side gains; Tunguz's second profit lever alongside pricing.
