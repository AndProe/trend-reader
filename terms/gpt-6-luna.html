GPT-6 Luna

first seen 2026-W39 · 11 links · 1 min read

OpenAI's cheapest GPT-6 tier, released 2026-09-22 at $0.10 and $0.50 per million tokens — a 50 percent cut on top of the 80 percent cut its GPT-5.6 predecessor took in July, which OpenAI states is permanent rather than promotional. Berman treats it as the week's best value, reaching 66.6 percent on Frontier Code at 22 cents a task against Astra's $1.70 at low effort, while OSWorld shows the widest gap to the frontier at 53 against 73 percent.
