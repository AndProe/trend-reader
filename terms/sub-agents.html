Sub-agents

first seen 2026-W30 · needed by 1 · 13 links · 1 min read

Parallel agents spawned inside a run under a coordinator, now too many to read and the source of bursty serving traffic; dozens with bounded concurrency in Latent Space's Astra practice.

Also seen as subagents, subagent fleets, sub-agent fan-out

How the sources use it

  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-21) his explanation for why they underperform: the parent must pass state that costs as much intelligence to summarise as to read. Cross-source (Gergely Orosz, SemiAnalysis, Tomasz Tunguz).
  • Sibo Zhu, Shicheng Fan, Xinyue Wang et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-14) cross-source; curriculum, actor and verifier agents run as a coordinated multi-agent system.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-12) Astra trained end-to-end for multi-agent orchestration.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-09-10) routed to cheaper models for smaller tasks at Uber.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-07) the unit that multiplies wave three. New for this source; cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Latent Space).
  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-03) dozens of bounded-concurrency subagents under one coordinator.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-19) parallel agents spawned inside a run, now too many to read.
  • Tereza Tizkova (Technically, 2026-08-06) parallelism as the thing agents do that humans only claim to.
  • Paul Iusztin (Technically, 2026-07-21) orchestration, with the warning not to reach for multi-agent systems early.
