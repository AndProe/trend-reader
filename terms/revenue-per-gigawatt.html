Revenue per gigawatt

first seen 2026-W32 · 8 links · 3 min read

Data centres are sized in megawatts and gigawatts because power, not floor space or chips, is what limits them. Revenue per gigawatt asks how much money each gigawatt of capacity brings in each year. Current estimates put frontier API serving at over one hundred billion dollars per gigawatt-year, and at the megawatt scale roughly fifty million dollars of revenue against ten to fifteen million of cost.

For an executive the metric does two jobs. It sets the bar: with twenty-five to seventy gigawatts of US capacity planned, each gigawatt must earn a defined amount for the build-out to pay off, which is the basis of the AI revenue requirement. And it explains behaviour: a lab facing that revenue per gigawatt will accept costlier on-site power rather than wait years for a grid connection, and a chip vendor will try to own more of each AI factory because the revenue per gigawatt is captured wherever the stack is integrated.

Also seen as revenue per megawatt, $/GW/year, revenue per GW

Deeper dive

What it measures. Take an operator's annual AI revenue and divide by the utility capacity that produces it. The metric works because the whole stack scales with power: a gigawatt of capacity implies a fixed number of accelerators at a given Performance per watt, which implies a fixed token throughput, which at market prices implies revenue. Modelling therefore runs both ways. Forward, from a chip generation's performance per dollar to the revenue and profit a utility gigawatt can support. Backward, from the capacity being built to the revenue each gigawatt must earn, which is how a national build-out figure becomes an AI revenue requirement.

Why it matters now. The numbers in circulation are large and internally consistent. Frontier API serving models at more than one hundred billion dollars per gigawatt per year; the megawatt-scale unit economics show roughly fifty million dollars of revenue against ten to fifteen million of cost, which is the source of the sector's Gross margin claims. Against those figures the cost of behind-the-meter generation is small, which is why labs accept it rather than queue for Grid interconnection. The same arithmetic underlies projections of very large AI revenue for a space company planning ten gigawatts of capacity by 2027, and it is the yardstick by which a chip vendor measures how much of each AI factory it can own.

Trade-offs. The metric assumes the capacity is fully utilised and that token prices hold. Both are uncertain: utilisation depends on demand arriving on schedule, and frontier pricing has been falling per token even as volume grows. It also flatters integrated players, since revenue per gigawatt rises when the same firm captures the chip, the system and the model margins, and it says nothing about the capital cost of the gigawatt itself, which sits in a separate line. Treating Compute as currency makes power the reserve asset, and the metric inherits every risk in that analogy.

What to watch. Realised rather than modelled figures from public operators, the gap between frontier-API and commodity-inference revenue per gigawatt, and whether falling token prices outrun gains in performance per watt.

Gross margin

Gross margin is revenue minus the direct cost of serving, expressed as a share of revenue; in AI it is the number that says whether selling tokens is a business.

Performance per watt

Performance per watt measures how much useful AI output, typically tokens or benchmark-weighted intelligence, a system produces per unit of electrical power.

AI revenue requirement

The annual AI revenue the industry would need to service its data-centre debt and capital expenditure, derived at $1.2 to $1.5 trillion from the debt load and put at $2.5 trillion in a widely cited independent estimate.

6 linksconcept

Compute as currency

Compute as currency is the view that once models become commodities, advantage is decided by access to compute and energy rather than by access to any model, and that compute is increasingly traded, for revenue shares or training tokens, rather than simply sold by the hour.

10 linksmetric

SpaceX AI revenue

SpaceX AI revenue is the projected income from the company selling compute capacity, forecast to overtake its entire space business by the fourth quarter of 2026 and modelled to reach three hundred billion dollars of annual recurring revenue by the end of 2027.

3 linksconcept

Grid interconnection

Grid interconnection is the process, run through a grid operator's queue, by which a data centre or power plant is connected to the utility grid.
