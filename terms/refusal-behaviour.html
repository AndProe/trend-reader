Refusal behaviour

first seen 2026-W36 · needed by 1 · 9 links · 1 min read

How models decline requests and what moves them: door-in-the-face sequencing in the arXiv paper, system-prompt prohibitions in Willison's read of Claude's rules.

Also seen as refusals, safety refusals

How the sources use it

  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-21) Almeida's argument that a refusal is a type error inside a dependency, and that safety alignment belongs to first-party products, not APIs. Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI, Frontier Labs, Simon Willison, Zvi Mowshowitz).
  • Andrew Anogie Uduimoh, Hadiza Umar Yusuf, Oluwafemi Osho (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Cross-source (Simon Willison); models that refuse generic harmful requests are reported to comply under domain-specific framing.
  • Diba Afroze, Xingli Zhang, Yazhou Tu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Simon Willison, Frontier Labs); refuse is one class, and False Executes are the failure to refuse.
  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-10) reseller platforms route refused prompts to more permissive models; the lab says its classifiers forced bio work onto weaker models. Cross-source (Willison, arXiv).
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-09) refusal-adjusted bio scores and safer higher-risk completions.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-04) safeguard false positives improved; several mundane-harm categories regressed.
  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-02) the arXiv door-in-the-face paper's term; here the refusal rules themselves.
  • Til Jordan (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-02) how models decline and what moves them.
