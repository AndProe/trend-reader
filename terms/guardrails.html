Guardrails

first seen 2026-W30 · 8 links · 2 min read

Controls that limit what a model or agent may do. Paul Iusztin treats them as the permission layer of an agent harness, balancing autonomy against babysitting, while Nathan Lambert's reading list cites Florian Brand's argument that closed-model guardrails are bypassed routinely.

Also seen as closed-model guardrails

  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-09-22) model safeguards versus company security, and inference providers as the weaker layer for open models. Recurring;.
  • Nikita Agarwal, Nivedit Jain (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-22) Cross-source (ChinaTalk, Interconnects, Latent Space, Zvi Mowshowitz); action denials at failure-preceding states are the restrictive half of the policy set.

  • Boris Wetzk (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-21) Cross-source (ChinaTalk, Interconnects, Latent Space); the validity conditions are the runtime-checkable subset, by the author's own checking asymmetry.
  • Abbas Raza Ali, Muhammad Ajmal Siddiqui, Moona Zahid (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-18) Cross-source (ChinaTalk, Import AI, Interconnects, Latent Space, Technically, Zvi Mowshowitz); part of the frozen configuration under test, so the guardrail is evaluated with the model rather than around it.
  • Diba Afroze, Xingli Zhang, Yazhou Tu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) cross-source (Technically, Zvi Mowshowitz); the paper's finding is that the model-level guardrail is not enough on its own.

  • Latent Space (Latent Space, 2026-09-16) most applicants have them and they do not work well; the standard checks existence, AIUC tests effectiveness. Cross-source (arXiv cs.AI, Interconnects, Technically, Zvi Mowshowitz).

  • Yunhao Feng, Ruixiao Lin, Ming Wen et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-14) cross-source (Technically, Interconnects); the paper trains a runtime guard model rather than a static one.
  • Seyedakbar Mostafavi (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-12) cross-source; capability-based access control and Dual-LLM isolation are guardrail mechanisms.
  • Nathan Lambert (Interconnects, 2026-09-11) used in this note; see Evidence.
  • Jordan Schneider (ChinaTalk, 2026-08-24) classifiers and refusals as the administration's whole safety approach, and the reason he thinks open weights break it. Cross-source (Zvi Mowshowitz, Interconnects, Technically).
