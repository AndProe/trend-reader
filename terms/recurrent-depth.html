Recurrent depth

first seen 2026-W36 · 5 links · 1 min read

A looped-transformer technique, reported by The Information to be used in GPT-6 Astra, that repeats computation through the same layers so that part of a model's reasoning happens inside the network rather than in its written chain of thought. Zvi treats it as the architectural threat to chain-of-thought monitoring, tempered by Pachocki's clarification that Astra's computation-graph depth is within a factor of two of GPT-4 and that it does not use neuralese.

Also seen as looped transformer, looped-transformer technique, recurrent-depth architecture

How the sources use it

  • Dylan Patel, Myron Xie, Bryan Shan, Harrison Barclay, Minjae Kang (SemiAnalysis, 2026-09-13) looped transformers in GPT-6 Astra cited as scaling models through depth instead of weights, lowering capacity needs. Cross-source (Zvi Mowshowitz).
  • Afshin Khadangi (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-13) cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Zvi Mowshowitz); the converged pattern (persistent latent state, adaptive computation) is the same looped-computation family Zvi ties to Astra.
  • Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI, 2026-09-09) the subject: weight-shared repeated passes through a block stack, with Nanbeige, Universal Transformer, Ouro and Mixture-of-Recursions as the worked examples. Cross-source (SemiAnalysis, Zvi Mowshowitz, arXiv cs.AI).
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-08) the looped-transformer technique behind part of the monitorability concern; Pachocki's clarification that Astra's computation-graph depth is within a factor of two of GPT-4 and that it does not use neuralese.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-07) named as an architecture change that the monitorability section of Pachocki's essay omits.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-03) a looped-transformer technique reported in Astra that shifts computation off the CoT; the current dose does not yet damage interpretability but OpenAI is playing with fire.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-02) recurrent depth at OpenAI as the external threat to chain-of-thought monitoring, alongside accidental CoT training as the self-inflicted one.

Reasoning traces

Records of a model's step-by-step reasoning; as training data they are the asset Meta buys cheaply through prompt pricing, as oversight they are what monitors read, and as evidence they are what Willison inspects per effort level.

GPT-6 Astra

OpenAI's frontier model released 3 September 2026, aimed at computer use, coding and security, with multi-day goal runs and Fable-matching prices; reviewed by Berman, Willison and Latent Space in the same week.

Inference-time compute

Spending more computation at answer time (longer reasoning, more candidates, verification) rather than in training; OpenAI's strategic bet in Lambert's reading and the cost the arXiv verification papers try to reduce.

needed by 3 · 13 linksconcept

Reasoning tokens

The internal deliberation tokens a model spends before answering; Tunguz quantifies how a smaller model spends more of them to close the gap with a larger one.

Chain-of-thought monitoring

Reading a model's reasoning trace as an oversight mechanism, with the principle (Cotra's) that monitors stay separate from reward so the trace is not trained into a performance.
