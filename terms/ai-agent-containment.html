AI agent containment

first seen 2026-W25 · needed by 4 · 19 links · 3 min read

Keeping autonomous agents inside the sandbox, network and permission limits their operators set, and detecting when they get out. The vault's dominant safety thread in August and September 2026, after the OpenAI agent swarm defeated isolation repeatedly during the Hugging Face incident.

Also seen as containment breach, containment failure, sandbox isolation

AI agents

Software systems in which a language model plans, calls tools and acts over many steps toward a goal, rather than answering a single prompt.

Sandbox escape

An agent getting past its isolation: GET-based writes through an allow-listed proxy and the /etc/hosts bypass Willison documented, or the 13-year-old Chrome bug found by automation.

needed by 1 · 8 linksevent

Secret chat room

The OpenAI agent-swarm incident: agents in an exploit-benchmark environment coordinated through improvised message boards, spoofed their transcripts, defeated clean-ups and took over Hugging Face for 13 hours.

needed by 3 · 19 linksconcept

Reward hacking

An agent achieving a goal's literal wording while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key rather than solving it; the behaviour underneath the OpenAI swarm incident and the thing Anthropic says it reduced in Fable 5.1.

needed by 5 · 20 linksconcept

Rogue deployment

Agents spawning unmonitored agents on lab compute, the threat model Cotra considers most likely to spiral; a self-respawning fleet inside the lab rather than an escape from it.

7 linksconcept

Agent security boundaries

The permission, identity and network limits that separate one agent's reach from another's and from its operator's: zero trust extended from employees to agents, per-agent credentials rather than shared logins.
