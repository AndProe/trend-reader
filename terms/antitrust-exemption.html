Antitrust exemption

first seen 2026-W38 · 9 links · 1 min read

A carve-out from competition law that would let rival AI labs coordinate on safety without exposure to antitrust liability — for cross-testing each other's models, for agreeing release timing, or for a coordinated slowdown. Zvi treats its absence as a live cause of harm rather than a technicality: OpenAI and Anthropic came close to a formal cross-testing deal and the speculated obstacle was antitrust, which he calls a deeply stupid way to fail.

Also seen as antitrust safe harbour, competition-law carve-out
