Open model adoption metrics

first seen 2026-W32 · 4 links · 1 min read

Measures of how widely open-weight models are used: downloads, derivative models, inference tokens and usage share inside companies. Nathan Lambert's Interconnects builds them into the Artifacts Hub and Adoption Dashboard, including a time- and size-normalised download metric, while the Forward Future team cites usage-share figures such as AT&T's and a US rise from 10 to 58 percent of usage in a year.

Also seen as relative adoption metric, open-model adoption
