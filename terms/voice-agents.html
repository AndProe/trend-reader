Voice agents

first seen 2026-W35 · 9 links · 1 min read

Agents that take spoken input and act through tools or on physical systems in real time, rather than answering by voice alone. Google DeepMind's Gemini 3.8 Live positions them for complex tool-using work measured on τ-Voice and EVA-Bench; the arXiv vehicle-authorization paper benchmarks how safely they authorise commands that move a car.

Also seen as voice assistants, speech agents

How the sources use it

  • Diba Afroze, Xingli Zhang, Yazhou Tu et al. (arXiv cs.AI, 2026-09-17) LLM-driven voice assistants acting on physical systems.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-15) the lab's target use: agents that complete tasks by speech.
  • Google DeepMind (Frontier Labs, 2026-08-26) the streaming variant is built for voice agents and live captioning at sub-second latency.
