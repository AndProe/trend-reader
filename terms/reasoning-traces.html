Reasoning traces

first seen 2026-W36 · needed by 2 · 9 links · 2 min read

Records of a model's step-by-step reasoning; as training data they are the asset Meta buys cheaply through prompt pricing, as oversight they are what monitors read, and as evidence they are what Willison inspects per effort level.

Also seen as reasoning trajectories, thought traces

Related terms

technique

Chain-of-thought monitoring

Reading a model's reasoning trace as an oversight mechanism, with the principle (Cotra's) that monitors stay separate from reward so the trace is not trained into a performance.

needed by 1 · 10 linkstechnique

Post-training

Everything done to a model after pretraining (RL on environments, fine-tuning, preference data), now the whole recipe for frontier gains because the public web is exhausted and where open-model activity has moved.

needed by 4 · 17 linksconcept

Reasoning tokens

The internal deliberation tokens a model spends before answering; Tunguz quantifies how a smaller model spends more of them to close the gap with a larger one.

6 linksconcept

Data flywheel

Inference usage feeding model improvement that attracts more usage; in Tunguz's ads-model post the mechanism Meta buys cheaply by pricing prompts as data.

7 linksconcept

Vision-language models

Models that take images and text together and reason over both, the model class behind computer-use agents that read screens and behind visually grounded reasoning research.

9 linksconcept

Data labeling

Human annotation of training data, a roughly $10 billion industry that reasoning traces from real usage now bypass, and the work Meta reassigned engineers to before its exodus.
