Zero Data Retention

first seen 2026-W34 · needed by 1 · 10 links · 1 min read

The enterprise contract norm that a vendor never retains or trains on customer prompts, now priced as a surcharge, offered by OpenAI and contrasted with Anthropic's inspectable safeguards; the term shared by Tunguz, Orosz and Berman.

Also seen as ZDR, zero data retention policy, no-retention tier

How the sources use it

  • Anthropic (Frontier Labs, 2026-09-10) abused by a reseller to hide gain-of-function traffic; the lab argues retention is needed for observability in biology. Cross-source (Tunguz, Orosz, Berman).
  • Simon Willison (Simon Willison, 2026-09-08) the unanswered question of what labs mean by using customer data to improve models.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-09-05) the adoption barrier Zvi and Roon (quoted in AI #183) think explained Fable 5's 11 percent share.
  • Ben Thompson (Stratechery, 2026-09-04) the retention walk-back as the week's most interesting move; Thompson attributes it to competitive pressure from OpenAI. Cross-source (Tunguz, Orosz, Berman).
  • Matthew Berman (Matthew Berman, 2026-09-03) OpenAI's ZDR versus Anthropic's inspectable safeguards; shared term across all three sources.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-09-03) (ZDR) — the enterprise contract norm that vendors never retain or train on customer data; now priced as a surcharge.
  • Tomasz Tunguz (Tomasz Tunguz, 2026-08-31) strict enterprise policy that vendors keep no prompt data.
  • Zvi Mowshowitz (Zvi Mowshowitz, 2026-08-27) Anthropic's enterprise custody compromise and the 11 percent Fable share.
  • Gergely Orosz (Gergely Orosz, 2026-08-19) the enterprise setting SpaceX cited as protecting customers.
