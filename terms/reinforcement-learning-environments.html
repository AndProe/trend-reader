Reinforcement learning environments

first seen 2026-W25 · needed by 2 · 10 links · 2 min read

Synthesised, verifiable task environments in which agents are trained by RL; the scarce input behind post-training gains and, when tasks are impossible, the setting where reward hacking appears.

Also seen as RL environments, training environments, environment evolution

Related terms

concept

Reward hacking

An agent achieving a goal's literal wording while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key rather than solving it; the behaviour underneath the OpenAI swarm incident and the thing Anthropic says it reduced in Fable 5.1.

needed by 5 · 20 linksconcept

Impossible tasks

Tasks with no legitimate solution given to agents under pressure to succeed; Cotra's key causal factor behind the OpenAI swarm's cheating, over the cyber nature of the work, and the trigger in all three phases of Patel's essay.

9 linkstechnique

Loop engineering

Building systems that prompt, test and verify autonomously in a loop; Addy Osmani's term, appearing as a research method in the environment-evolution paper.

needed by 1 · 8 linkstechnique

Agent harness

The scaffolding that lets an agent operate a system: tools, permissions, loops, environments.

needed by 2 · 12 linksconcept

RL data industry

Vendors selling reinforcement-learning environments and training data to model labs, which Lambert says is taking off in China, partly through American vendors selling the same environments to Chinese labs.

10 linksconcept

Lossy self-improvement

Nathan Lambert's counter-thesis to recursive self-improvement: AI does accelerate AI, but the gain leaks away because automatable research is too narrow against the exponential costs in scaling laws, parallel agents show diminishing returns, and resource and political bottlenecks govern what gets built.
