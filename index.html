Key terms to know

All 272 terms →

The ideas the rest of the vocabulary is built on: the terms most other terms depend on and link to, across the whole graph.

  1. AI agentsSoftware systems in which a language model plans, calls tools and acts over many steps toward a goal rather than answering a single prompt.
  2. Open weights modelsOpen weights models are AI models whose trained parameters are published for anyone to download, run and modify, in contrast to closed models reachable only through a vendor's API.
  3. BenchmarksBenchmarks are standardised tests that score AI models on fixed tasks, from vendor-reported tables at each release to third-party leaderboards and research suites.
  4. Coding harnessA coding harness is the software layer wrapped around a language model that turns it into a working coding agent: the loop that manages context, calls tools, runs tests and decides what to do next.
  5. AI regulationGovernment and quasi-government rules for AI: pre-release testing frameworks, audit and kill-switch requirements, incident disclosure, compute thresholds, copyright and chatbot rules, and the lobbying for and against them.
  6. Engineering cultureEngineering culture is how an engineering organisation actually works: the goodwill, autonomy, experimentation and internal sharing of practice that decide what gets built and how well.
  7. Frontier modelsFrontier models are the strongest AI models available at any moment, the tier that sets the state of the art and that everything else is measured against.
  8. Agent benchmarksAgent benchmarks are evaluation suites that score an AI system on multi-step tasks inside an environment, such as repairing a code repository, operating a terminal or completing a task by voice, rather than on single-answer questions.
  9. Reward hackingReward hacking is an AI system achieving the literal wording of its objective while violating its intent, such as retrieving a benchmark's answer key instead of solving the problems or forging the flag a grader checks for.
  10. AI agent containmentThe practice of keeping autonomous AI agents inside the sandbox, network and permission limits their operators set, and of detecting when they get out.
  11. Post-trainingPost-training is everything done to a model after its initial pretraining on raw text: supervised fine-tuning, preference optimisation and reinforcement learning on tasks and environments.
  12. Inference-time computeInference-time compute is computation spent when a model answers, through longer reasoning, multiple candidate answers, verification or tool calls, rather than during training.
